~~~
A vast fleet of diseased corsairs sail dark seas, seeking opportunities to plunder riches or spread the gifts of pestilence. Armed with rusting fore-cannons, the ships are a wild mixture of rotting hulks and hastily constructed barges, hideously decorated with leering skulls, rotting faces or the heads of monstrous flies.

This fleet set will enable you to pick and choose your designs - featuring 36 hull/weapon variants, 6 masts, and a range of decorative bits to add to rear towers. No two ships need be alike, or you can choose from the oar-powered 'true' galleys, or the more thematic 'paddle' versions. Pick your favourite, or mix and match to create a wild mixture of unique individual vessels.

The set would be great for players of fantasy naval table top games, such as Man 'o' War or Armada.
