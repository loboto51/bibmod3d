Description :

~~~
This model is the result of a sleepless night spent asking ChatGPT how a ball-shaped tank works. That idea led to the need to design a "ball tank" for my Squats (WIP), and then I suddenly remembered the Necromunda Squats trikes...

A few design tweaks here and there, and the result is what you see. Since I wanted it to serve as a proxy, I included the same weapon options as the original trikes (boltgun, meltagun, and two autogun options), plus a small retractable stabilizer wheels because reasons. 

Note that the wheel/tire has a small arrow; this indicates the slightly flat side of the wheel that must be glued to the base.
~~~
