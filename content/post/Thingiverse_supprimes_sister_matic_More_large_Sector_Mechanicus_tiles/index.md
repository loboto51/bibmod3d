So you want a large Sector Mechanicus tile, and you don't want greebly detail? And, maybe, you have a fairly small resin printer, so printing it in one piece isn't really possible? OK, or maybe you have a big enough printer?

Here's a few options for 122mm octagonal or square tiles. They match the dimensions you would expect for Sector Mechanicus. They have all the join-y holes underneath. 

Unlike [my other large tile offering](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6962622) these have no locators on top. The bolts are recessed and do not protrude at all (not even like regular SM tiles), so you can stack whatever you like on top.

Your choice whether you want a port in the middle (and what orientation) or not.

If you are printing tiny little parts on your tiny little printer, then you will want:
* 1x centre piece, whether that's a square or a port
* 4x outer section, suited to port or not as appropriate
    Technically you can mix the oct (non diag) and square sections, I guess, if you want an unusual shape
    (e.g. if you were making [a big platform](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7092618), say)

If you don't care about splitting up your tile, then just print one of the full ones, or a quarter/half. These are designed to stand alone as regular tiles. If you can fit it on your printer, it'll be easier than assembling 5 parts to make a single tile!

There's a zip file named "fdm" containing all the whole-tile pieces with no recesses (except the join-y holes) underneath. I have _not_ tested them on an FDM printer; I do not own one. It just seemed maybe useful.
~~~
