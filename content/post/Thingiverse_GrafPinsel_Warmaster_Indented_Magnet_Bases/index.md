Description :

~~~
These are a 40x20mm base for troops and 25mm round base for characters, both with indents to accomodate strip bases. They have sloped edges on their lower halve, so you can pick them up more easily.

Intended for resin printing or FDM printing with water-soluble supports.

You can fit up to two Ø 3x1mm neodym magnets on the underside. The holes should be pretty tight and only need glue to hold the magnets. They still have a bit of wiggle room though, in case your material shrinks during printing.
~~~
