Based on Lord_Chronos Tiny Space Humans - Main Tank [https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/game/epic-scale-imperial-guard-main-tank]

Which was in turn based on Mkhand_Industries Epic Scale Lemoine Russel Main Battle Tank [https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/game/epic-scale-lemoine-russel-main-battle-tank-mkhand_industries]

My friend is a BIG fan of the Epic Bombard, so I thought I'd try to get one to scale with the amazing Tiny Space Humans - Main Tank from Lord_Chronos. Since the advent of AT and AI, I have upscaled my Epic efforts to 8mm scale, so that's what we have here. If you want this for 6mm, please print at 80% and you should be good (I have not tested this though!).

- Sides/tracks and top hatch lifted directly from 'Tiny Space Humans - Main Tank'
- 3 variants included; 
--Standard
--'with Anchor' - I'm not sure on the real terminology here
--'with Anchor and Crane' Crane was flimsy on it's own, so I have it resting on the anchor

EDIT: Normals corrected on cannon supports

Please let me know if you print it and how it goes for you! I can make edits if needed.
