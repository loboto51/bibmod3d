Gun pods for miniatures! Designed to fit into a standard launcher / heavy rocket holder. 
This pack includes Long (30 mm) and Short (10 mm) length gun barrels, as well as different rockets/missiles heads, harpoon, some flash hiders for multi-barrel  guns, blank head and even adapter for gun barrels from "Puma/Viper conversion kit".  
All of the heads are mounting on one of the four bodies - long and short gun pods, bomb body or gun body.

Update V1.1: added new type of barrels length - Medium(20 mm) with a new medium gun pod, three new styles of barrels (HMG and T-Rex-style machineguns and AGL short barrel), adapter for Hunter buggy and two types of drum magazines (for gun pod (Drum-Flat) and gun (Drum) body). Also, 7,62 Hex minigun barrels is fixed for all three lengths.
