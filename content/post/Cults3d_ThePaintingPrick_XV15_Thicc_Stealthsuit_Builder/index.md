XV15 Thicc Builder

V1 Update: The amazing Possi seperate the Knee, Shin and Foot, so they have been included in the file instead of my boot-leg hacked version! I've slightly updated the Knee 'connector' to smooth out different posing options. 

This is a download for a .3mf file (generally workable in MS 3d Builder) for a slightly converted version of @Possi's Amazing XV15 Decomissioned Stealth Suits. LINK: https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/game/space-communists-decomissioned-xv-15-stealth-suits

They are a fantastic and faithful rendition of the original models, I just need something a little bit bulkier. To do this I used some Crisis Legs (also by Possi) to 'enthiccen' the thighs, while scaling up the calves and feet. I also created a more armoured left arm, as well as adding in a variety of different weapon systems, as well as a sub-system backpack mount. Added in a 'workable' collar, neck joint as well as helmet seal, with a mid-section and posable (in 3d builder) upper thighs, knees etc. 

The colour parts are left in there to show they aren't 'attached' to the model in any proper way, but are left so you can easily remove them as needed, they are nominally there to just bulk out the ratchet and ghetoo cuts I made to free up some pieces for posing. 

You'll also find a couple of differently posed hands, as well as some hands that are broken up into individual finger pieces and palm, to allow you to pose up your own hands.

In the builder you'll find one of these thicker Stealth Suits in a variety of pieces, allowing you to play around with it and create different poses and load outs. 

Just to note, this is not really a 'set and forget' file, its for people who have some level of basic (such as myself) 3d tool using abilities.

If you have an questions or what not, please hit me up over on Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/thepaintingprick/

Good Luck and Enjoy!

Parts List:
Anything not listed here, is directly from Possi's XV15 Decomissioned Stealh Suit or just whipped up by me.
