Description :

~~~
This model was in my backlog and I wanted to take a stab at it but the old Rodin was pretty rough and I didn't want to continue. Eventually I found the MZ4250 had made one of these but not after trying this on the older version of Rodin so I wanted to try it again. I think it turned out pretty nice. Like other I posed this and did some detail work. I made the shield and sword separately (the AI sword was no good so I made it from scratch then just did touch ups on the shield). The AI did most of the outfit and I think it looks really nice - I just filled some holes, carved in some details and cleaned up the geometry a bit.

I've added the sword and shield to the model here in case someone wants to remix them in another model.
~~~
