Description :

~~~
This is a set of weapons I modeled in Blender, referencing the 3.5 Edition D&D Player's Handbook weapon illustrations; if they look weird, that's how they were drawn. :) 

!These are not meant to be printed out by themselves! They are relatively low-poly and are meant for use with your own custom role playing miniatures, through use with Blender, Meshmixer, Zbrush; whatever you're comfortable with. OBJ and STL files have been provided.

The top row in the picture is modeled as shown in the book; these may be unsuitable for immediate use with your models, especially with FDM. I urge you to use these as starting points to modify at your leisure! Scale them appropriately for your project.

To that end, a -thick- variant of each weapon is also supplied, where they have been expanded in both the X and Y axis, and in some instances hand tweaked to make them meatier and more substantial.

More of these will be coming! Let me know in the comments what you might like to see, and if you use these I'd love to see your work!
