"Suported Dwarf The Second by Dopaminis" by Leighbar: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4424041

While looking for a Troll Slayer to use in Warhammer Quest I found this great Demon Slayer model, which Leighbar has rescaled and supported. Unfortunately the original seems have disapeared along with its creator. I have used meshmixer to remove the supports to restore the original version so you can support it yourself and rescale it freely.

Leigabar notes their version was "scaled down by +/- 8% so he would be on par with WizKids Nolzur's and DeepCuts minis."
I have kept it at that scale so if you want the original size you'll need to scale it back up to ~108.7%.
