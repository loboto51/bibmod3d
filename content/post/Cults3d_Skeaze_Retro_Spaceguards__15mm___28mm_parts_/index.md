Description :

~~~
Sci-fi troopers inspired by the 80's classics!

Currently included are lasguns, flamers, meltas, and plasma guns. There are 2 torso variants; one based on the original cover art by John Blanche (the vests), and one based on the actual models (with armor plates over the chest).

All the 15mm pre-assembled models are not merged, so you should be able to easily separate and customize them.

The separate parts will almost certainly NOT fit together if you print them separately. They are intended to be assembled digitally first.

Scale: The 15mm miniatures are a little more on the "heroic" side, about 16-16.5mm at the eye. The fits with many popular 15mm manufacturers, but if you want more "true scale" you should shrink them to about 95%. I have no idea about the 28mm scaling. I don't really own any 28mm miniatures any more.

Progress: There are currently only retro-style weapons, and open faced helmets. I'm planning on doing bare-heads, fully enclosed masks, and more modern style weapons very soon!
