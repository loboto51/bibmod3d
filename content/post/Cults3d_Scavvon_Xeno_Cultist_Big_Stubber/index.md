~~~
There is something about cultists of all kinds, they just love their slug throwing stubbers, especially the Big Stubber.  What can you say, it's just iconic? Would any of them really be worth calling cultists if they didn't have their Big Stubbers? I've decided to build an army of Frateris Militia led by an Apostate Cardinal.  Let's be honest, Frateris Militia are basically imperial cultists. And as such, they will need their own Big Stubbers, lots of Big Stubbers.

I started with LoggyK's excellent Heavy Gun for this design and I just kept adding details until I had the look of a xeno-cultist Big Stubber. The final model is sort of a mix of old and new. The scale should be 1-1.

This model wouldn't have been possible without the talent and generosity of LoggyK.
