This is a reupload as the original wasn't showing up under my designs, and I also wanted to add another version, with the sword in a resting position and a bionic arm.

"He looked over the horizon, he'd grown accustomed to the ash wastes of Teidon Prime, just like he had with the endless swamps of Delta-64-91. He'd been on countless worlds, each a different home, with different families. Families he'd soon depart with, as he once again would be the only one out of the warzone. He gave a fake smile to the corporal next to him, who smiled back unknowingly that the sergeant had done the same to last 3 men and women assigned to him. "another warzone corporal, another battle to win. Remember your training, and I'll make sure you'll be back home with your family in a couple of months". He donned his mask and yelled orders for the charge, already having said goodbye to those around him"

This is a single model, meant to represent either a veteran sergeant or higher ranking officer of the Hostile Environment Guardsmen.
He's armed with a powered sword, plasma pistol and improved armour against shrapnell.

Remixed from the following parts:
Heroic scale heads for wargaming miniatures 28mm. By Brexit: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3217411
