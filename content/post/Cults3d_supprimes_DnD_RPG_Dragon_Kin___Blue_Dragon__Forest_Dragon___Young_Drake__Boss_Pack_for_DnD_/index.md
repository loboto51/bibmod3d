Three dragon miniatures inspired by Blue Dragon, Forest Dragon and Baby Dragon styles. One huge adult dragon, one medium forest dragon and one smaller young drake, plus a scenic egg cluster.

Models included

Blue Dragon – large dragon on a 75–100mm round base. Body slightly coiled, wings half-spread, long neck arched with head lowered in a threatening pose. Horns swept back, scales sharp and angular. Base with rocky outcrop and cracked ground.

Forest Dragon – medium dragon perched on a fallen tree trunk crossing the base. Wings partially folded, tail curving around the log. Head turned to the side, antler-like horns and leafy frills along the neck. Roots and mushrooms on the base.

Young Dragon – small, compact dragon standing on a 40mm base. Wings folded close to the body, head slightly raised as if curious. Shorter horns, smoother scales. Simple rocky base.

Dragon Egg Cluster – scenic piece with three large dragon eggs partially embedded in rock, sized for a 30–40mm base; can be used as an objective or terrain.
