Description :

~~~
Simple add-on parts to modify any diecast car for any postapocalyptic racing game. Scales well with ~1:64 original Matchbox scale. Primarly designed for Gaslands but can be used for any other games as well.

Updates
---------
* **12.7.18:** Started to split this thing into multiple new things with a theme (armour, fuel tanks, etc.) for easier maintenance...
* **23.5.18:** Some very simple straight rams added (PS: forgot some that are not on the photo...).
* **22.5.18:** Motor Prototype added, prints in 3 parts to avoid overhangs (no support needed). Parts will be interchangeable in the future to allow customization and different builds.
* **17.5.18:** Some simple exhaust pipes added...
* **10.5.18:** Barrel and gas can set added. Barrels are very, very thin (1 0.4mm layer) to be able to dent them after printing with a little bit of heat...


Additional elements will be added in a timely manner...
Splitting into separate theme-based things (barrels, rams, armour, motors, etc.) was requested but this will take a little bit of time in reorganization.
~~~
