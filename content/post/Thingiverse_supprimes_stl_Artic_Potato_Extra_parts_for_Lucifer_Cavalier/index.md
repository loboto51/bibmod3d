~~~
Been learnig to use blender and decided to make some extra parts for HighCommand's great Lucius Knight model. They are all scaled for Adeptus titanicus, but scaling it to 350% should be the right scale for 40k.
I am planning on making more.

The fitting for the arm might be a little tight, so some sanding is recommended.

If printing the cerastus lance and shield in AT18 scale, you can use a piece of filament as the articulation pin, if printing at 40k scale, a pin model is included, just up it to the same scale as the other models. Might need some sanding either way though.

The tentacle was remixed from this model https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/art/horny-tentacle-big-mech

For some reason, if you print the chainsaw on it's side, the mesh breaks, not sure if it's a Cura only problem.

Removed the Plasma Decimator and Volcano Lance as I am making new ones to replace the removed, horrendous, old ones.
~~~
