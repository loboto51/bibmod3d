I wanted some physical tiles to keep track of my campaign and have a big one coming up, so thought lets make some.

Here's some interlocking hex tiles that you can add details to make a map of territories 

Print off the hex tiles and add the walls and columns to make them unique, id recommend about 8 walls and pillars for each tile

They have small hole sin the top for adding miniature flags names or pins to make it easy to recognise whose tile they are and a small nameplate to show what territory they are.

Contains 
22 outlands dustplains tiles
9 sump sea tiles
10 hive wall tiles
2 Plain tiles
chem pit
old Ruins tile
2 slag tiles
pillar, double wall and single wall.
some outlander scenery such as towers and stills

I plan to add some  more specific ones for the individual territories but no promises.
