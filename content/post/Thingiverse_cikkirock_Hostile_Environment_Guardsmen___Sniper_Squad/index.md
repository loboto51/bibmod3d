Description :

"Each crackle of their rifles, was a death sentence to their enemies. Their old rifles had been replaced for this mission, and the soldiers had been eager to try out the fresh forge-crafted laser rifles. The normal magazines had been exchanged for a power cable, hooked up to the proto-fusion capacitators on their back; standard issue for their regiment; allowing them a much higher rate of fire, and fewer reloads than normal"

3 different variants of snipers intended for 28mm wargaming, in line with the rest of the Hostile Environment Guardsmen I've made, and also includes a standalone sniper rifle. Due to popular requests, I made sure that the cables were much thicker and wider this time, to make them easier for printing and painting.
This is also one of the few times I'll be using legs (by ZioCru, go check out his stuff), as I intend for the models I make to be used as conversion pieces, and not wholly printed models.

Enjoy!
