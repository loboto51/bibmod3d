~~~
When servants of the Inquistion must hunt heretics in the dangerous depths of the undercity it never hurts to have some extra firepower. That's were the Servile Cyborg with Sizable Gun comes in. This single-minded automaton can pack as much firepower as a crew served heavy weapon team. He never complains, but he rarely takes any initiative either. In fact, he performs best with a bit of guidance from his friends. He can be equipped with a selection of three devastating heavy weapons depending on your needs. The ODV Heavy Blaster launches bursts of high caliber dual-mode projectiles that are equally effective against armored or unarmored targets. One shot from this weapon is more than enough to deal with a measely chaotic cultist. The opposite end of the spectrum is the mighty Multi-Mazer Melta-Beamer. This weapon hits just one target, but it hits extremely hard! Definitely a waste when targeting a wimpy chaotic cultist, but hulking mutant abominations or vile places of worship for the followers are the Dark Gods are ideal targets. The final weapon option is the Phased Plasma Projector, this weapon is the happy medium of overkill. It hits like a cargo-8 and covers an area wide enough to catch multiple targets. As an additional benefit this particular Servile Cyborg's Sizable Guns each have unique enhancements to improve their reliability. Because let's face it, there is never a techno-guru around when you need one, let alone when you are in the heart of darkness battling heretics.
