~~~
Sector Mechanicus style tiles.   
Simpler - no details in the gratings. Sometimes you don't need all the detail.   
Many of them have pipe ports, which oddly don't exist in the originals outside a ruined one.   

Standard holes underneath for pillars and join-y connector-y things.

I needed a single rectangular tile with a port for myself. But, you know, with what I needed to make for that one, I could make multiple permutations. I'd done half the work already (It might seem like I'd done more than half, but making the permutations took way longer than I expected!).

1x1 square tiles, and 1x2 tiles, and a single strange 1x1.5 tile.  
Two different gratings (plain and with a cog design), plus pipe ports in different positions and numbers, with hatches at various orientations.  
Honestly, yeah, it's overkill. I do not know how many people care about getting hatches in the every possible orientation, but well, the person sitting in my chair really is that ridiculous.

The *FDM* zip file contains the exact same tiles, but without the recesses underneath. They still have holes for pegs; there's no other changes. I _have not_ printed these in FDM. I don't even have an FDM printer. It was a suggestion someone made to me, seeing as the simpler nature of the top detail might lend itself to FDM printing, and I figured, sure, why not. May as well, right? Perhaps this is useful to someone, perhaps not.

* What's with the 2-port tiles' letter-number designations? Why are some numbers missing?
    A/O/S = Aligned/Opposite/Symmetric. The numbers are missing because some would be equivalent (e.g. O1 == S1, A3 == S3), or creatable simply by rotating/mirroring an existing piece (e.g. A4 would be A2 mirrored)
