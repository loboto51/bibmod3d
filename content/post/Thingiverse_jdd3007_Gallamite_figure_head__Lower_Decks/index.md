Description :

~~~
My interpretation of the head of a Gallamite, as spoken of on Star Trek, scaled  to playmates and Mego figures.

To achieve the transparent skull look, print the heads labeled with brain in clear resin, you can pour pink watered down paint through a hole in the bottom of the neck then leave upside down for a few hours to dry. You may need to use a small pointy item to clear the shaft to the brain as resin sometimes creates a blockage.

The heads not labeled with brain are solid heads and can be printed and painted however you want.
