Description :

~~~
I love the 40K Ork Killa Kans from Thorlek (https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4612598). To make his set complete the models were missing a couple of things. I created the right arms for the models (https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4790967). The models included all of the different weapons options except Rokkits and the Grotzooka. So here they are (or at least my take). 

I printed the Thorlek's models out at 70% scale to better match the GW model sizes. These weapons are designed at that 70% scale. If you want to use them as the original size of Thorlek's models you will need to print them at 142%.
~~~
