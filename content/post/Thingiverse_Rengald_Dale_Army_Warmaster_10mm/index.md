Army of Dale led by King Brand and Bard II in 10mm Scale

This is an army of Dale using my collection of LotR SBG models in 10mm scale based on models produced by Games Workshop. These models were roughly based for use in Warmaster.                      Bard II of Dale
                     Captains of Dale (Foot and Mounted)
                     Warriors of Dale on Foot
                     Warriors of Dale Mounted
                     Archers of Dale
                     Knights of Dale on Foot
                      Knights of Dale Mounted
                     Windlances

NOTE: These models have not been test printed
