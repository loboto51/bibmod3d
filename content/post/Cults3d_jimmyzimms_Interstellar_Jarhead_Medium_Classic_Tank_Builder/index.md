Provides a version of Woddish's excellent TINY MEDIUM TANK CLASSIC CRUISER that has been sliced into major parts that are enabled to be quickly swapped and edited for customization by changing visibility of components between different options for support turrets, sponsons weapons, hatches, symbols, and to make Sicaran based vehicles (Accelerator, Punisher Omega, and Arcus currently).

This is intended for designers and modelers to product their own faction customized vehicles from in a standardized and high quality form. Handy How-To guide included.

New part contributions are welcome!

Base tank from Woddish with KJdidit's additional turrets rescaled and normalized (plus cleaned up) for use on the main tank.
