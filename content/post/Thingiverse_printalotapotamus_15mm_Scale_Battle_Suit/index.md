Description :

~~~
This is based off the excellent mech by [NathansMind](http://www.thingiverse.com/NathansMind/about).

I rescaled it for 15mm play and broke it into pieces to make printing easier at such a small size. It's about the size of a "dreadnought".

I typically print it all in one print with all the pieces laid out as they are with support. Then I clean off the support and assemble it with super glue.

I have also made some great "bits bots" using the legs from this model and the torso/weapons from what ever spare parts I have laying around in my bits box.

An all around great model!

Note: example.stl is not intended to be printed. It is just for reference.
~~~
