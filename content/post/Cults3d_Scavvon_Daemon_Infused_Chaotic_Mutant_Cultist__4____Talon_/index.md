~~~
The cultist who would go on to be known as 'Talon' was scrawny and puny. He was constantly being bullied by the local thugs and gangers in his poor neighborhood. He joined a chaotic cult in hopes of a better life and to escape the foul treatment of his tormentors. Alas, he found that he had only traded one set of bullying torturers for another, for in the chaotic cult strength and power were prized and weakness reviled. The cultist sought out the legendary Master of Possession, Faustus Daemoncaller who was at that time visiting his world in secret. Daemoncaller was only too happy to perform the dreadful and perverse ritual upon the young seeker.

Once joined with the daemon it learned of his past experience and history. Perhaps it was compassion that made the daemon within him transform his body into a massive and brutishly muscled thing with one oversized arm ending in a savage claw. Or perhaps the daemon sensing the dark emotions of it's host, his rage, hatred, and sense of weakness that the daemon fed upon and transformed the young cultist into a form ideal for enacting bloody revenge on all those who had previously plagued him.

After that the cultist earned the name Talon based on his new form and his keen desire to maliciously destroy any who gained his ire. Not long after the old neighbor where Talon was raised became the site of a series of brutal and savage murders which continued for weeks only to end as suddenly as they had began. The unknown perpetrators, or perpetrator, was never identified or apprehended.
