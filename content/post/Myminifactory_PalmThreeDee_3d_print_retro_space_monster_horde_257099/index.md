Description :

~~~
Purely fan-made material. Everything is 100% my own creation.

I needed some retro-style space monster for my derelict spacecraft game that I have been building as 3d printed version. As I've printed my board with 20x20mm squares, I needed some smaller scale models with some beefier arms and legs so that I could print them with my FDM printer, I decided to sculpt my own models.

The alien creature I sculpted in Blender, rigged and posed it for 12 different dynamic poses and you can mirror them so you get 24 different poses in total, like I did with mine.

NOTE: The models have been scaled for my 20mm scale game. So they are quite small. You might want to try out scaling them up to match regular minis. With 135% scale they should be close enough. I suggest to make some test prints first to get the scale you want.

I have printed them successfully in 100% scale with my Prusa Mini+. Used 0.2mm X/Y expansion in advanced options to thicken them a little bit more. I printed them upright, using standard PrusaSlicer supports. Used 0.07mm layer height.
