### Intention of the Design
A Heavy Phosphor Blaster alternative / replacement for the good old Kastelan Robot of the Adeptus Mechanicus.


#### Motivation for the design
I thought, this beefy guy really needs a gun that that is just as impressive as he is, and the original one...well...definitely not beefy enough.

#### Magnetization
The "hole" or indentation where the arm would "go in", is made for a 5x1mm magnet disk, so that you can magnetize the arms and switch between the Gatling, the original gun oder the big ol' fists.

#### Why is it marked "work in progress"?
It's marked "work in progress", because I intend to add another variant of the Gatling blaster when it's finished. The items you can see here and download are already finished and won't change.

#### Scaling necessary?
Scale should fit the original miniature, compensation for possible material shrinking during print might be necessary.
