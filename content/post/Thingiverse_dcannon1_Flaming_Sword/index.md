Broke out the sword from the amazing Bloody Glorious Hawk Boy model to use as a spiritual weapon for a DND Cleric character and got a lot of requests to post the STL. Recommend printing on a resin printer. If you have a really well dialed in FDM printer you can try printing the file as it's imported with supports to have the best chance of getting the flames to come out. Might not be a bad idea to scale up to 110 or 120% for FDM to give the fine details a better chance.

Once printed simply glue it to your favorite base model which can easily be printed in FDM or resin. Here's one of my favorite packs:
https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3675552
