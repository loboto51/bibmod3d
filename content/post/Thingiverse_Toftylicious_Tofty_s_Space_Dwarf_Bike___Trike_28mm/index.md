Description :

~~~
I present my Space Dwarf Motorcycle and Tricycle models.

There are various possible arrangements that these vehicles can be built in:
Bike with chopper or moto front forks and bolter light or indicator light.
Trike with different front forks, lights and either the large or small rear wheels.
Bike with Sidecar, with different forks and lights.

The bike is designed for the seated biker metal space dwarves, from a well known table-top wargaming company in the late 1980s.

The front forks and light part should be interchangeable with the original metal space dwarf bikes, so can be used as replacements for lost or broken parts if needed.

The twin bolters on the headlight part are a slightly modified version of MrOrangejumper's Bolters for space dwarf bikers models:
https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3737197
~~~
