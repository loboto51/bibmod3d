~~~
Recreation of the long out of production Rebel Grot Cutta model.

First try at something like this, really happy with how it turned out.

I drew the chassi in paint.net and then extruded them to make the basis of the chassi, cleaned that up in tinkercad and added detailing and precision stuff.


To complete this you need: 
The Chassi.
The steering cog+handle.
A front wheel.
Two back wheels. 
(ive include two variant wheels, one that looks like the old school and another that looks nicer IMO)
The Crows nest.
The mast+sail support, these two parts are available separate or joined together and then split down the middle for ease of printing.

EDIT: Ive added the shape of the sail as a separate file as well as split mast+sail support with slots for the sails and another version where the sail prints as part of the split mast+sail support.

A peice of 3mm rod of some kind, for use as a wheel axle for the front wheel, i used a bit of bbq skewer.


I looted the wheels from these things:
28 mm heavy artillery - https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:2629202
Freaks of Speed ork buggy - https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:2902496
Scrap Trukk - https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3028389

And the cog for here:
Gears for custom cog work - https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:2218127
~~~
