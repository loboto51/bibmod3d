Description :

~~~
This is a set of 10 new and unique fantasy / sci-fi tabletop plants, designed specifically for building detailed miniature bases and terrain pieces.

After examining this species for quite some time, I noticed nothing dangerous about it. It thrives in a wide variety of environments and seems to do nothing but look beautiful. However, there are numerous reports of people sleepwalking toward fields of these plants at night. There must be some kind of attractant being emitted by them. Allegedly, entire villages have been wiped out when the wind blew from an unusual direction, carrying the plants’ attractant across the canyon. Many residents sleepwalked straight toward the field and fell to their deaths...

**What's included:**
- 10 unique plants
- Every plant is available as an individual STL file (for printing just the ones you need)
- One additional combined file containing all plants arranged in a single STL (if you want to print the whole set at once)
- Suitable for both FDM and SLA/resin printing
- Depending on your needs, the plants can be scaled up or down quite a bit.

There are two versions of these models:
One version with and one without a hole on the bottom. (Which makes painting a lot easier as you can have a plant on a stick!)

Like most of my other plants, these print just fine without support.

**Btw. I'm always thinking about new plants and other terrain stuff. So if you have any suggestions, just let me know.**
