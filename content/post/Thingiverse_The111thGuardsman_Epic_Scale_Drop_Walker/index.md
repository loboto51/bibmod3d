This is a human Drop Combat Walker for Airborne troops. Provides heavy fire support to the troopers on the ground where there would otherwise be no support available, or they would have to rely on close air support.

Remixed from another file and upscaled 10-15%. The pilots are from Wakes Emporium's drop troopers pack, and not included in this file for copyright reasons. I also slightly upped the feet scale to make them less likely to break.

ROUGHLY 8MM SCALED! Check 

I highly recommend adding your own pilots before printing as shown in the images above.

Printed on a Resin Printer, Photon Mono 4k at 0.05mm layer height.
