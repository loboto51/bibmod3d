~~~
Check out my Patreon! I'm over at http://www.smokalotapotamus.com

15mm scale Havoc Brothers of the Plague Brother detachment.

This set has a pistol and energy axe as their armament.

The axe is separate because it snaps off if printed vertically. For best results, orient the axe horizontally parallel to the print lines, a few mm off the print bed with supports. This will help it to bend but not break when the model is being handled. I used CA superglue to attack it to the model and it is quite sturdy for as small and thin as it is. You will need 4 axes for the 4 models. I left it solo for you but when I printed it I printed 4 axes at a time.

I left these models as overlapping geometry instead of combining them with booleans so that others can more easily remix this work into other poses or new models entirely.
~~~
