I've wanted a cool Martian wasp to go with my Mars Attacks game martians from the first time I drew the giant bug event card in the game. I've modded the front claws to be pincers claws like all the other Martian bugs and gave him a nastier mouth pincer. Also thanks Simonsonson for the cool claws that I used for the basis of my claws and mouth.

Printing it tonight, hope other people like him.
