Description :

~~~
An easy-to-use Blender tool for creating procedural grimdark Sci-fi floor tiles for Epic-scale gaming.

Fighting on grassy fields or rocky planetoids is all well and good, but when you've captured all the enemy's cows and mined all their minerals, sometimes you need to take the fight to their hive cities and starships!

Including:

- Continuous Supports for high quality vertical printing with next to no support marks or post-processing required. Print a whole city's worth of tiles in a single print run!
- A variety of tiles, bracings, conduits and rivets. Select the styles you like, or add your own!
- Optional orthogonal tile bracings and a Retro "9 small tiles" layout to suit the floor tiles found in a Workshop, or Forged World

**Can I scale this up for non-epic gaming?**
Maybe, but the proportions for these tiles are exaggerated to suit small-scale gaming and would probably need to be toned down for larger scales.

**Are you going to make more terrain builders?**
I might have a few other things in the *pipe* line!
~~~
