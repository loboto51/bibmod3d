Description :

~~~
Cyberpunk spy miniatures with a good old 90's look at 32mm scale for your wargames (A, B, C, D, and E models).





This is a pack with all files from Cyberpunk spy models A to E (also available individually).





This is a 4 parts minature, wich requires assembly (and painting! ^^) once printed. You will need to print :


- a body (choose one)


- a head (choose one)


- 2 arms to choose from the 60 options. Each arm file references the name of the weapon, and a L or R suffix indicating a _Left or _Right arm. NB : 2 handed weapons (shotgun, lasgun, autogun) need to pick the corresponding right and left arm.





Tip: All parts are reversable (mirror / symmetry) before printing for even mor pose options! :)
~~~
