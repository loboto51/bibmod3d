~~~
I decided to open the Neo-Retro Marine project up to everyone. Please share anything you make with any of these files! I love to see what you do with them! If you publish anything made with these files, you can release them for free, or PWYW with no minimum price.

The parts are NOT really designed to be printed separately. The idea is to assemble and pose everything digitally, and then print it as a completed model.

Many of the pieces have been slightly tweaked and improved from the original versions I used for the marines I originally published. I definitely recommend scaling the legs down to 95%. The originals were definitely a bit too big compared to everything else.

I've included multiple "builder" files, which include loads of different options for various details and alternate parts. With all the various head, torso, and leg options, you could theoretically make thousands of unique marines.

I've included a PDF in the .zip file with some instructions. If you're familiar with Blender, it should be pretty easy. If not, hopefully my instructions are actually somewhat helpful in getting started.

The files will make a marine that is approximately 20mm tall by default (a very large heavily armored super-solder in 15mm/1:100 scale). Although you can scale the parts up by ~165% to match the classic 28mm marines. You can even scale parts differently to make more realistically proportioned marines for larger scales!
~~~
