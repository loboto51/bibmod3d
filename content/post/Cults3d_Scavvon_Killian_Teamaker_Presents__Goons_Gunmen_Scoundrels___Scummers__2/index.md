This is the second Scummer I've remixed. He makes a great random NPC or supporting character for your role-playing games or miniature games. Don't get me wrong, he could be your character and an epic hero at that. Though he was made to be a bit generic featuring: a rifle/carbine, light armor, and a knife. Scummer #2 here also has a bionic eye and a bionic leg, which can be treated as just cosmetic details. Unless of course, you want them to do more in your games.

Just for an extra bit of fun and functionality, I have included a small random name generator for your scummers in the images above. You know the drill, roll D66 on each of the name tables and then use that to come up with a suitable name. Of course, these tables and the names on them are all just for inspiration. Feel free to pick the names you want, to add your own names to the mix, or change the names in any way that you want. This name generator is a tool, not a box.
