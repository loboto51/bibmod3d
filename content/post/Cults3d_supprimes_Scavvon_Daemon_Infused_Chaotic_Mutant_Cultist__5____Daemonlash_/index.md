~~~
The chaos cultist who would go on to become known as Daemonlash was a solitary and taciturn individual.  Despite his reserved manner this cultist was a skilled warrior and a gifted leader.  One day he challenged the leader of his chaos cult to a fight to see who would lead the cult.  He was handily defeated by the current cult leader.  A certain amount of shame came with being thus defeated, but a more serious problem came with this development.  The existing cult leader now saw this cultist as a potential threat, maybe he would make a challenge again one day and win.  Everyone in the cult knew that their cult leader would almost certainly maneuver things so that his most recent challenger was never a threat again.  Knowing this, the defeated challenger volunteered to become a living host for a warp daemon.  As a barely sane living weapon for the Dark Powers the former cultist was no longer a threat to the cult leader's position, but at least he was alive.

The daemon that possessed the cultist who would become known as Daemonlash had a peculiar way of not making any outward changes to his host's physical body.  Only when in combat would the daemon transmute his host's flesh in the form of grotesque fleshy tentacles that suddenly burst from the cultist's chest.  These tentacles shot forward with explosive speed to entangle, rend, and strangle his foes.  At these times the cultist's head would also transform to a ghastly cackling daemonic visage.  And thus was born the daemon-infused mutant cultist know as 'Daemonlash.'
