~~~
Spaceship set made to match the Tau Kor'Vattra (Commerce and Exploration) Fleet models.

Models are based primarily off [Rzupan](https://cults3d.com/en/users/Rzupan) models, with some pieces taken from the Italianmoose's [Phoenician Modular Fleet](https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/game/tau-modular-fleet-capital-ships), then adapted to what looked good to me.

I had the models printed by a 3d-party service, and first ones are even painted.

If I was doing it again, some of the engine casing and hangar bay casing might be better thicker, and orientation on hangar bays of Explorer and Hero should be fixed - although some people might enjoy the assymetry.
~~~
