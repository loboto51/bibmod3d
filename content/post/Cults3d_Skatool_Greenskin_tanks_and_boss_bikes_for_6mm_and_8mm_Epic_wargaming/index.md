Description :

~~~
Having been dragged into another hobby, this time in 6/8mm scale, and missing models I wanted to field I decided to model my own. As I like my greenskin models a little bigger than usual, they are more like 10mm, but hey - they're supposed to be bigger than usual 'oomies ;)

These have been printed on a resin printer and did fine (as you can see in the images). I include painted versions with the skorchas being WIP, but I will see to that soon.

In the collection you will find: 
1. A tank with a long gun, great for laying down direct fire - the turret is separate from the body so you can point it straight in your opponents face ;)
2. A tank with a big gun, for sending shells overhead and smashing multiple enemies at once. There are some accessories added so no two look the same.
3. Two mobile flamethrowers - one 2-nd World War Willis style, the second a tankish ignition machine
4. A big boss on a bike with lots of dakka
5. A regular boss with an equal amount of dakka
6. A BIG WAGON with even more guns. This one has three turrets to choose from so you can customize them to your liking

Update!
7. Added three different leaders in heavy armour and some solid waponry
~~~
