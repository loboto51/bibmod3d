Description :

~~~
These are small 2.5 inch snap together hex terrain tiles with rivers, a flat plains tile, and flowing river tiles with mountains - 11 designs.

I want to give full credit to BigMrTong for a majority of the work before I even get started into further details. I did ask his permission before making the changes and additions. 

One of the first changes I made was to resize the miniature terrain to 2.5 inch tiles across. I would not have posted if this was all I did. I have left those tiles here for download. I also did some clean up and remeshing to my own preferences. 

The big changes I made which was easier with the larger tiles is to make these snapable to each other using a standard openLOCK connector at any side of the hex. I wasted some filament getting these set just right so that any direction can snap and not have space between hexes. I did my trigonometric calculations wrong a few times before I got it right.

The connection clip can be downloaded for free here.
https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:2343306/files

What this allows you to do is have a display of the terrain that the roleplayers can see and decide what directions to take or follow. A sort of high level overview of your developed lands. Since the lands snap together, you don't have to worry about placing them in the same order between campaigns nor bumping the table and losing the order. You do have the ability, however, to move the tiles relative to each other and snap it in different places to create different scenery or add to the existing terrain as the party explorers further along.

In these pictures I added flock to the tiles to give it a more realistic view, but you could also just paint them to reflect your environment.
~~~
