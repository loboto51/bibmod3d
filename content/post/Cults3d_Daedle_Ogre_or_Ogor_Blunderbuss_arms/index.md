Description :

~~~
Ogre arms carrying blunderbusses. These are semi compatible with Games Workshop/Citadel Ogre or Ogor models but they are not a perfect fit. They require greenstuff work to help blend them into the GW Ogre body.

I recommend using a hair dryer to warm the pieces slightly before trying to put them on the model or they may snap. 

To repeat: THESE REQUIRE MODELLING WORK TO GET THEM TO FIT. THEY DO NOT FIT PERFECTLY FRESH FROM THE PRINTER.

There are 3 poses of arms - two carrying the gun and one firing it.

There are 6 variants of the gun (guns without the arms also included). Each arm pose has a version with each gun.
~~~
