A powered fist for all your guardsmen needs! scaled for the rest of the Hostile Environment Guardsmen, or as a spare / upgrade for your existing plastic models. Does not include any iconography, as it is easy to add your own with either transfers or a 3d program.

The Powerfistv2Fixed is the intended file for regular use, as it is a complete model, and cannot be posed or taken apart in any way. The "not-fixed" can be taken apart and modified in a program such as blender.



Powered fist and parts by me, built over (and remixed from) Ziocru's Angry Spaceguards Arms Clean: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4580213

Example picture of a model you could build includes parts from Ziocru, BREXIT, and LoggyK and remixed and added parts by me.
