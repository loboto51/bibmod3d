Description :

~~~
Epic scale open-topped/scout chicken walker to represent your favourite sentinel support. 

Parts to make an open-topped scout sentinel with three additional cab variants with extra stowage, two leg options, matching 20mm bases (with two 3x1mm magnet holes) and a jungle fighter pilot. Weapon options for rapid laser cannon and heavy flamethrower. Chainsaw accessory crucial for all Deathworld missions.

Supported Lychee file included.

These were bashed together with some shoddy additional bits from myself. The real credit for the base parts goes to:

- Walker body and legs remixed from [Tdoesepic's Epic Scale Sentinel](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3737581)
- Pilot remixed from [Jazzmantis' 10mm-wasteland-townsfolk-male-civvies](https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/game/10mm-wasteland-townsfolk-male-civvies)
- Stowage bags from [KubFire_cz's Ultimate WW2 Stowage project](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:5189324/files)
- Jerry can and tarps from [Treadhead Rad's Vehicle Stowage and Accessories Set](https://www.myminifactory.com/object/3d-print-vehicle-stowage-and-accessories-set-220755)
- Stowed laser gun and smoke launchers remixed from [Solutionlesn's Vehicle accessories](https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/game/vehicle-accessories)

Thanks muchly y'all!
~~~
