~~~
I'm currently working on a desert terrain project. Unfortunately, using alien and fantasy plants doesn't really make much sense in this case. ;-)
So, I launched Blender and started creating desert plants like cacti, succulents and dry branches, instead. Things might have gotten slightly out of hand... so now I have 140 of these prickly beings lying around here... and you can too!

**What's included:**
- 140 unique plants, organized as 14 different types with 10 variants each
- Every plant is available as an individual STL file (for printing just the ones you want)
- Suitable for both FDM and SLA/resin printing
- There are two versions of each model. One with a **flat** bottom and one featuring a little **hole** for inserting a toothpick, which makes holding/painting these much easier.

These models print **without supports**, just like most of my other plant designs.

If you're building desert or steppe landscapes, adding a cactus or two (or 140) is always a good idea. I hope that you'll have a lot of fun making these grow.

Happy printing!

**Btw. I'm always thinking about new plants and other terrain stuff. So if you have any suggestions, just let me know.**

--
All my models are made by human-made. No AI involved!
If you enjoy these, please consider liking the set, leaving a comment and posting a make - I'd really like to see how these work out for you.
**Want to know what's next? Follow me on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/GGreeble). :-)**
