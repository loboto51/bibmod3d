Do you like hearing you foes die screaming from hot plasma death?
Do you think that there should be more plasma weapons on your Tiny Titans?
Do you think the only good Enemy Titan is a Dead Enemy Titan?

If the answer to and of the above is YES! then I have something for you!

This Carapace Mounted Plasma Blastgun for Tiny Reaver Titans should put a real sparkle in the eyes of your foes!

Remixed from JB's Wardog Plasma, a Carapace Turbolaz and a few odd shapes.


Includes both an unsupported single piece and a pre-supported single piece weapon with magnet holes to suit 5mmx1mm rare earth magnets. Note the Supported version prints fine on my Photon at 0.03mm layers but it is likely the thin supports used may not work on larger printers like a Saturn.
