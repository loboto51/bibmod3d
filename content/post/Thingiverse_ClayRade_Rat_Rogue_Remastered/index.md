This is a re-detailed remaster of an [earlier miniature](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3669594)! 

"Rat Rogue! He's ready to plunder the nearest dungeon of loot, sack at the ready to haul it back in! Perfect for homebrew races like Ratfolk, Ratkin, etcetera."

My first sculpt was fairly early on in my Blender learning experience, and I like to think that I've improved a little bit since then, so I decided to add a little more detail to it. Some superfluous clothing folds, better looking belt pouches, a few details here and there, you get the idea. The earlier version looked fine printed with FDM, but now that I have a resin printer I've realized it needed a little more spice!
