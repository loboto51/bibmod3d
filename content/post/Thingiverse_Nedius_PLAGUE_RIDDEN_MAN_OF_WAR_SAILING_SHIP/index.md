A diseased and decrepit hulk, this ship is believed to be cursed with demons of disease, plague and famine. Crewed by worshipers of dark gods, this ship leaves foul waters in its wake, and fear precedes it like an oncoming storm.

A fantasy themed sailing ship suitable for naval wargames and table top games. 

This set comes as a kit, with options for sails or no sails, and with some alternatives for the trebuchet armaments. There is also an option to do a hull without the fly-proboscis bow sprit, which could be printed separately or replaced with a more standard sailing ship sprit (also included).

If you want a ship closer in scale to those of a game with a very similar name to this vessel, print at about 80%.
