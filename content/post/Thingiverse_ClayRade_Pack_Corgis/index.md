~~~
This stalwart and sturdy Corgi is here to join you on your adventures!

These models are designed for use with tabletop roleplaying games, such as Dungeons and Dragons or Pathfinder, but it is detailed enough to scale up and print as you please.

As is, it is scaled to fit on a 25.4mm / 1 inch round base, not included. The 3-Headed version is scaled for both Medium and Large bases, so print him as large and terrifying as you want!

Two versions of the four standard Corgis are provided: Heroic, and Small. Small is scaled to be close to 28mm accurate, and Heroic is scaled to be larger and easier to print. If you have any doubts as to which to choose, go for Heroic!
