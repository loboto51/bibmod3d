Description :

~~~
My first real attempt of a remix of a model.  I took off the string, added faces to the left over holes, and put a larger cylinder over the arrow to make it fatter.  I tried printing it as is with my Form2 at highest setting and the string and arrow were still way too small.  I hope this works.  

*Update
Using Blender I was able to remove the string and fix the holes that it left behind on the bow and her hip.  I then used Meshmixer to select each side of the bow, the shaft, and the arrow head, and extruded along the Normal.  Did 4 test prints throughout the process with my Form2 using Tough1500 resin.  I am VERY PLEASED with the results.  The bow and arrow look a little too big on the computer, but when printed at 40mm they are perfect.  Attached are the updated files that I used.  Enjoy, and a big thanks to Toawi for uploading the original model. 
~~~
