I'm about to launch a SciFi Kickstarter (linked to above) and - like all my projects before it - I have developed a set of bases for the minis in the campaign. Since I love the community, I release tem all for free. These are styled the same as all my previous bases - which are unique in that they have a bevel and "ring" on the bottom - designed to allow you to easily paint a black (or colored) ring on the bottom, as your brush follows the bevel. I personally like this as it makes the minis pop on a board.

This collection includes a bunch of 1" and 2" round bases in the style of scifi and cyberpuni, and includes:
