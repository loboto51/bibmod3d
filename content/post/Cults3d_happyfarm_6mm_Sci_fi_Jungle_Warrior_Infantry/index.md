Description :

~~~
Epic scale jungle fighter troops to represent your favourite Deathworld regiments of the imperial guard. The pack contains: 

- 5x Troopers with laser rifles
- 1x Flame trooper
- 1x Sergeant with chain sword and pistol
- 2x Bonus, spikey spitting cacti as base fillers

Supported Lychee file included with some mirrored for poses of the basic troopers for additional variety.

The base models are adapted from [Jazzmantis' Gamma Grunt infantry](https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/game/wastewars-gamma-grunt-infantry); thanks muchly. Gyro-jet pistol and flamer remixed from [Jimmyzimms' Space Nuns collection](https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/game/space-nuns-combined-collection); thanks to you also.

I hope to add command characters and heavy weapons troopers in the future.
~~~
