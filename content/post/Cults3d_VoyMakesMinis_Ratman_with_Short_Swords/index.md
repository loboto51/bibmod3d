A free miniature for all of you to enjoy. 

This little rat miniature fits on 25mm bases and prints as a single, presupported piece with minimal post-support artefacts that can be easily sanded down.

A bit of a behind-the-scenes:

Sculpting characters with accurate proportions is always a challenge to me, unlike basing bits and terrain elements I always focus upon. But this one came up pretty good. Also, my adventures with Nomad Sculpt continue. The blades were first modeled in Blender, as I prefer it for hard surface modeling.

I also started learning how to draw a few months ago, although for the background of this mini, I simply line traced the already sculpted miniature.
~~~
