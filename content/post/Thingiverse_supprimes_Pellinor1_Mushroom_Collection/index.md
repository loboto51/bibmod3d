Description :

~~~
The versions ending on 'v' are for attaching to a vertical surface.

They were made with 28mm Miniatures in mind,  Personally, I use them at 80, 100 and 125% scale. At 80% the stems get very thin, but the print still succeeds on my anycubic photon.

All parts are separate shells, so it should be easy to extract individual mushrooms for remixes.

No additional support needed. These files were made for a resin printer and are most likely too delicate for FDM printing.

Please like, please comment, please show your makes! I'd love to see what you build out of this. 
~~~
