Recreation of the long out of production Rebel Grot Big Lugga.

Goes together pretty easily, i split the frame for the splattapult and the wurky bits on the back so one can get the details on both the front and back.

The Boarding planks gets the teeth glued on at a 90 degree angle.

I added a set of (imo) nicer wheels with thread for right/left/front/back, also including classic style wheels as an option.
