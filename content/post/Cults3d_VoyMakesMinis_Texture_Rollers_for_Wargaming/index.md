Description :

~~~
Here are 5 (so far) texture rollers for your wargaming board. I plan to make more textures, but so far you get:

- Cobblestone
- Cracked dry earth
- Decorative wall stone
- Long Bricks for walls
- Wooden Planks

Print the rollers on a resin printer to get the best quality of your textures. I tested these on an FDM printer and they look fine, so if you decide to go with an FDM printer, print the rollers in a vertical position.

The rollers come with a handle. Print the handle flat on an FDM machine. Use 0.28 layer hight to speed things up, and 20% infill. Place supports below „hinges” that hold the roller and you’re done. 

You can place the roller on a handle quite easily due to the spring action of the handle.

UPDATES:

- 07.02.2023: added 2 new rollers, one for long bricks that can be used for walls, and one for wooden planks. The wooden planks one isn't too deep nor detailed, but still I add it because someone may find it useful.
~~~
