[The second set of added tiles for my Hexquest World Mapping System! Click here for the original post and much much more information!](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3728353) 

Be sure to scroll to the bottom for additional tiles from other Thingiverse users!

Expansion 2 includes the following: 
-Arena: A battle arena / colosseum 
-Army Camp: A series of tents with supplies, and a big officer's tent at the end.
-Bandit Camp: A fortified settlement, surrounded by log walls.
-Lighthouse: A lighthouse on a coastal tile
-Standing Stones: A stonehenge-esque stone circle, complete with sacrifice pit in the middle. Druids and stuff!
-Stone Ruins: Some old fort made of stone bricks, all busted up.
-Swamp: Stinky swamp filled with plants and stuff
-Tower Fortress: Some kind of weird tower fort, possibly of doom.
-World Tree: A really big tree, for really big adventures! Possibly filled with Mana, a baby -Yggdrasil, or you could paint some tired eyes and a mustached mouth on it.

Also included are three variants of the original Lake tile with entrances/exits to connect to River tiles. 

These are little 1-inch hex tiles made in a low-poly style, roughly inspired by stylized overworld sprites from JRPG world maps! They should be easy and fast to print on FDM, requiring no supports. The scale is abstracted, don't worry about it!
Ideally printed with a .3 mm nozzle for small details!
