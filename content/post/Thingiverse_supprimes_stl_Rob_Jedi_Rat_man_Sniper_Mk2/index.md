~~~
Was at a gaming club meet on the weekend and my friend's Skaven got smooshed again and he was talking about how crazy priced jezzails are compared to how many people want to use and the amusing conversions people do to make their own so as they say when you have a hammer everything looks like a nail, I have a 3D printer so I went looking on thingiverse to find one to print and found the original work of VidovicArts. Since I can't leave anything alone I played with his design a bit to add more detail to the gun using the flintlock mechanism from Harrowtale's Miliatia Gunner and added a bunch of rivets and expanded the bands around the furniture and redid the scope to look older and more like a brass telescope and tweaked the shield with more rivets and a notch for the gun at top and flipped the logo the right way around, also made the head a bit bigger cause I like my ancient Skaven models with their wide heads. Also loved the bandoleers of powder charges on old metal Skaven so made my own bottle/pouch and added a heap of them around his waist. Hope you like my version.

Edit: forgot about the belt buckle logo so flipped that too and made the one of the shield poke out a touch more. Also did a test print at 0.12 layer height, with line supports and it prints great. 


original designs by GamesWorkshop
~~~
