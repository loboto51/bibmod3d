Ork Ravager Attack Ship for the long out-of-production game Battlefleet Gothic.

I did not design this model. It was extracted from the computer game Battlefleet Gothic Armada 2. I have done quite a bit of fixing and pruning of small parts to make this model printable at the proper scale.

A version with a base attached and a pre-supported version is included in the download.

I recommend printing on a resin printer.
