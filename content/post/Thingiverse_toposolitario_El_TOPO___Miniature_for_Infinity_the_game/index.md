"El TOPO" is a miniature for the world of INFINITY (the game) that dates back to 2007, when Corvus Belli's Infinity was still young, there were no 3D printing (not even for production masters) and the forums were starting to be active with just a bunch of guys in the spanish community.

El Topo was first found in Carlos Bostria's comic strips about the game. Later in 2008 I modeled (classic modelling with green stuff) a version of the miniature which was produced in very small quantity.
Now, this is actually my second try on creating a miniature with ZBrush, and I'm happy with the results, so you can get it for free!

Enjoy having a chubby mercenary in your ranks!
