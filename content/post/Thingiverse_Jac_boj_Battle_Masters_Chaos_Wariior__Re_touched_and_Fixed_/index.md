~~~
I have cleaned up and retouched this lovely old miniature. The original was limited by plastic moulding tech back in the day. I have helped give definition to some of the softer areas so that paint washes will take to it better. I have re-sculpted the skull and skull icons. I have removed the base and some mould lines. I have added definition to the chainmail and other various areas. Hopefully, once printed, these will still look like the originals, just a little sharper and cleaner!
