Here's a pack of Opossums I've sculpted for Tabletop Miniature use, or whatever you feel like! 

Contains:
-Opossum (Mouth Open)
-Opossum (Mouth Closed)
-Opossum wearing a pack (Mouth Open)
-Opossum wearing a pack (Mouth Closed)
-Opossum twisting and screaming
-Opossum twisting and screaming (with flag)
-Tiny little baby Opossum (really small, best printed in resin or with a 0.2mm nozzle. Glue them onto the bigger ones if you want!)
-NEW: A momma Opossum covered in 4 little Opossuminos

With the exception of the Little Opossum model, all of them are sized to fit on a 1 inch round (25.4mm) base.
