A dwarf tank/paladin
The detail featured on the shield is inspired by an architectural detail of an Art Nouveau villa in Lodz, Poland.

Suitable for 28mm tabletop games.
Created so that it does not need any supports and prints well on a cheap FDM 3d printer like the Ender 3.
There are variants with square and round bases.
