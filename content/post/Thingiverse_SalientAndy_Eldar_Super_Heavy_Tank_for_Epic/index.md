Description :

~~~
A model of an Eldar Super Heavy Tank for use in Epic and designed for printing on a FDM printer. I have successfully printed both the hull and turret using a Bambu A1 by rotating them 90° (so they are pointing vertically), positioning the models 5mm above the base plate and using automatic tree supports.

The vehicle is sized to be accurate according to the background lore (https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Scorpion).

This was developed from the amazing 28mm scale models by caddyh11 (https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/game/poncey-space-elf-hulking-big-tanks-builder), without which, this model could not have been made.
~~~
