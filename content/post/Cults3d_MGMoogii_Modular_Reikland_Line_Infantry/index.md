Modular Reikland Line Infantry

Reikland Line Infantry are the professional career soldiers form Reikland province of the Empire, they are volunteery and very well discipline unit. These soldiers are the main units that Reikland provides to the Empirium, other provinces can also request or hire them from the governing body of Reikland. 

In the regiment there are three ranks, Riflemen, NCO, Sergeant and ceremonial rank Drummer. 

My idea behind this is to modernized old Empire Hand Gunners, now they use armor and bolt action rifles. Company captains are now officers, Lords are now generals shooting their black powder revolvers instead of the flintlock pistols. Scale is very close to the old minis.
