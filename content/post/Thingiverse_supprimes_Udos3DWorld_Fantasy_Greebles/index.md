Someone recently said "there are no good fantasy greebles out there, only SciFi" and I checked and that's right. Unfortunately. Got me motivated and since I already have quite a lot of things, I decided to turn those things into greebles. So here you are, doors, windows and stairs to glue on any surface to create a fantasy house from it!

Seems like the stairs variants weren't uploaded here, so head over to cults for some stone stairs below your doors: https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/game/fantasy-greebles

Wargaming should be for everyone – not just those who can afford expensive files. That’s been my belief from the start, and it still drives everything I do. That’s why, despite the many hours of work behind each model, I’m releasing these files for free. No paywall, no gimmicks – just great stuff for everyone to enjoy.
