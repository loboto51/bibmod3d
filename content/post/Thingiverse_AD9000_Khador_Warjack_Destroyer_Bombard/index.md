Description :

~~~
The old Mk2 / Mk3 Khador Destroyer is now out-of-production. These Destroyer Bombards can be used to convert a Juggernaut chassis to a Destroyer (you will need an axe for the right arm, but you probably already have the Juggernaut axe right?). They are designed to be magnetised at the wrist with 5mm diameter magnets.

They are obviously not a replica of the original parts, but as can be seen in the photos they incorporate similar design elements, making them recognizeable proxies.

Warmachine, Khador, the Juggernaut and Destroyer Warjacks are all the intellectual property of Privateer Press.
