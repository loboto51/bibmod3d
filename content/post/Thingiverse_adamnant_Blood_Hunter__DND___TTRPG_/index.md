Here is a Blood Hunter miniature based on the artwork from Explorer's Guide to Wildemount. I had a request for one of these so I put it together and then realized the request was for a changeling Blood Hunter - whoops. I guess I've gotta go back to making another blood hunter model. Technically changelings can take any shape or form so maybe  this one works too. Aside from that there doesn't seem to be much for what changelings should look like in their unchanged form.

This was made with AI using a service called Rodin and I brought that into blender and sculpted some of the details into it, fixed a broken hand and other wonky topology.
