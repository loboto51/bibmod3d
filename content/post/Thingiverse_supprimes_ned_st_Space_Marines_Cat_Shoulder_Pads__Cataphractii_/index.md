~~~
A friend asked me to make space marines shoulder pads with cats (for Cataphractii and regular guys). 3d sculpts are rough because it was a quick project, and they were made to print it on "Anycubic Photon C" in small size that my friend asked: the first iterations were more thinn and refined, but the details were lost and the parts were too thin, so after 3-4 iterations I had to get this result. I don’t remember which version you can see on these photos (last or pre-last).

Cataphractii  shoulder pads were modeled by me, regular shoulder pad and cats were found on thingiverse.com:
https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3086045
https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:1378163
https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3171393
