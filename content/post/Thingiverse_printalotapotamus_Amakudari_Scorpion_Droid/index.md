Something I put together for my Gruntz games to glue onto a 25mm washer and stat out as a heavy vehicle specialist with the infiltrator perk and a plasma cannon. I am actually using a version without the barrel by printing it recessed then gluing in a 3mm plastic rod into it for a smoother stronger gun barrel than my printer can give me. I have included it both with and without the barrel in case you want to do the same.

Shoutout to MicroPanzer for making a great line of 15mm figures!
