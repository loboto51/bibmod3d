My set of 100 scaleable Fantasy Tabletop plants seems to have been well received. So, here are another 55. Like the previous ones, these new and unique fantasy / sci-fi tabletop plants, were designed specifically for building detailed TTRPG miniature bases and terrain pieces.

We had to crawl into the farthest corners, the darkest caves, and the most dangerous swamps to find the right samples. But it was worth it once again:

**What's included:**
- 55 unique plants, organized as 11 different types with 5 variants each
- Every plant is available as an individual STL file (for printing just the ones you need)
- One additional combined file containing all 55 plants arranged in a single 17×17cm STL (if you want to print the whole set at once)
- Suitable for both FDM and SLA/resin printing

These models print without supports, just like most of my other plant designs.

Whether you're creating jungle bases, alien worlds, fantasy forests, or toxic wastelands, I hope that you'll have a lot of fun making these grow.

Happy printing!

**Btw. I'm always thinking about new plants and other terrain stuff. So if you have any suggestions, just let me know.**
