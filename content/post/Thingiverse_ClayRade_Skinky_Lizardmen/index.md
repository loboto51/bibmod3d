From out of the jungle, Lizardmen spring forth! They look friendly though, don't they?

Here are two skink (the reptile itself) inspired miniatures suitable for tabletop roleplaying games. Best printed in resin for fine detail and delicate parts, but feel free to give FDM printing a shot, although I can't guarantee the best results.

[If you liked this model and want to show your thanks, you can buy me a coffee!](https://ko-fi.com/clayrade)

[And follow me on Instagram!](https://www.instagram.com/clay.rade/)


Three files exist for each model: one that is just the figure, one that is just the base, and one where the figure and base have been joined. I personally find printing the base and figure separately and gluing them together is the best for support addition/removal, but that's just me!
~~~
