Fish Person! This was another fast sculpt based on the sprites of a Mina The Hollower NPC, only known as "Fish Person". 

He's got a little bit of Innsmouth swagger to him though, don't you think? 

Feel free to scale him up and down as you please; I've pre-sized this model to fit onto a 1-inch round (25.4mm) base for tabletop use.

I've strategically sculpted this little guy to be printable in FDM with little to no supports, so it should be pretty easy.
