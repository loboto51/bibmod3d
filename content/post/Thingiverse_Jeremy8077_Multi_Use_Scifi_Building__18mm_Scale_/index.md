Using [Dutchmogul's Scifi Habs](http://www.thingiverse.com/thing:1435875) and TinkerCad I hacked up his beautiful habs to make this multi-use Scifi edifice. I'm using it for a makeshift clinic in our ongoing [Wayfarer](http://www.illgottengames.net/wayfarer/) campaign and have included a bunch of clinic stuff in the .stl files. This building is in 18mm scale, but could easily be converted to 15mm (scale down 83.3%) or 28mm (scale up 166.6%). 

We have [Kickstarted the original SciFi Habs](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/292543184/starchitecture-3d-printed-15mm-scale-sci-fi-citysc?ref=nav_search). Also, we have set up a [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/illgottengames?ty=h) page for anyone who wants to help us keep our toys and games free and open source.

This print was a ton of work in a short amount of time, but it'll be so fun to play with! That is until tonight when my GM wrecks it! Meh, that'll be fun too.

Thank you for looking, happy gaming!
~~~
