My chaos army still needed a kind of leader.
So I was thinking: Who could lead an army of Word Bearers?
Should be an Iconoclast converted to chaos.

I know the Gal Vorbak are regular space marines which are posessed by demons.
But I tried to design a Iconoclast which got demonic powers, but is no abomination of flesh and steel, yet provides massiv power and armor. 
So my orientation was more the look of a demon primarch in terminator armor.

The result is Scython my chosen Gal Vorbak Iconoclast, favoured by the gods of chaos for his work in corrupting his entire legion.
Scython with his black wings of hate, wielding the fire of chaos in his battle staff, will bring the truth of chaos and the word of Lorgar to all his enemies.
