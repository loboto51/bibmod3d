These OBJ models are designed for use in [Sculptris](http://pixologic.com/sculptris/) to give you a head-start creating human (or otherwise anthropomorphic) characters, and are what I use as a starter for many of my own miniatures. If you import them into [Sculptris](http://pixologic.com/sculptris/), their parts should remain separate and you can immediately begin sculpting and posing them any way you like.

If you make anything with these, let me know and post a link here in the comments section (and certainly post a remix when you have something printable). I'd love to see what you come up with!

You can find an expanding collection of compatible parts [here](https://www.thingiverse.com/dutchmogul/collections/desktop-hero-parts).

We've set up a [Patreon page](https://www.patreon.com/illgottengames) for anyone who's interested in helping to fund us so we can keep making free and open sourced games like Pocket-Tactics, Wayfarer Tactics. and others, not to mention the growing catalog of gaming miniatures that you can use for whatever you want! (Psst... we take requests and commissions...)

Enjoy!
