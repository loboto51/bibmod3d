~~~
Thingiverse is broken and I cannot add any current links to the remixes, as soon as it's fixed I will add all the information in. Sorry for the inconvenience. 

NOTE: Every part except the backpacks and helmets should be printed at about 85% scale. Do a test print beforehand to make sure that they are scaled correctly to a primaris. They shouldn't excede 40mm in height.

Inspired by the great art of Mo Mukhtar https://www.artstation.com/artwork/RYxAbO

I decided to give it a go and design a 3d printable model kit to host your very own Asta'Vesa soldiers. 

This time I am working only on my own idea with creating a Apothecary equivalent for the Asta'Vesa. These are one of the most important people involved in running an Astarted Force and I cannot imagine Tau Managing to sustain any astartes force without the Apothecaries maintiaining their medical needs. 

Ladies and Gentleman: meat the Apothecary.

They aren't as close to the fanart as I'd like but then again, they are designed to retain their character when being printed as a 28 milimiter scale models so I needed to exagerate some features and smooth out some details and I used some preexisting model sculpts and heavily modified them.

As always, please don't use this kit commercially. This is a gift from me to the community.
However if you would like to tip me, my tip link is https://paypal.me/elmutanto
But this is totally optional and the files are free to download and use non-commercially.

Hope you guys will like this. It was a blast to work on them.
~~~
