Original files can be found here:
https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/game/filius-ares-chain-axe-blades

I really like this kit by oHm, but I generally don't trust resin shafts to last. With their permission here is a remix that I've made.

The axehead is hollowed to accommodate a 1.5mm brass tube and the handles are hollowed to fit a 1mm brass rod. 

I've added supported files just in case. Small hollow bits are fiddly, so take special care when cleaning them up as they can form resin and cleaner traps and the bit will be ruined.
