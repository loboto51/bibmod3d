~~~
After the fall of the Council, the Great Hunt was unleashed against anyone who seemed fit for the arcane. During these dark times, the Assassins emerged as a force of balance. "Assassination" was the term by which the vulgar referred to an unresolved crime; an Assassin was that individual capable of committing a crime and never be discovered. Little by little, their presence was becoming more frequent during the Great Hunt: people who tortured children during the night appeared dead in the morning; judges who condemned the innocent were found hanged and with horrible marks on their bodies.

In her quest for balance, the Assassin searchs within the Dark Wizard's domains looking for the means to restore Magic to it's rightfull place... and becoming a more lethal weapon against all who opose the arcane.
