This was a fun little model - the Doomguard Rot Blade. It's a CR6 with a very nasty ability that, on kill, turns a creature and their non-magic items to ash. This is pretty brutal. Now I'm not sure on the ruling if that negates death saving throws as well but regardless it's nasty.

👍 Don't forget to Like and Collect this, so you can easily find it later! ⭐
If you like my models and what I do then come follow me on Patreon https://www.patreon.com/c/Adamnant for updates on my quests and access to exclusive minis.

This started as an AI model that I posed and sculpted, added spears, sword and helmet to. The helmet was something I had to kinda make from scratch. I think it looks pretty good. Here is a full video of my work on this model: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xc8zzAErLSc

There are 3x variants of this mini. One with a pointing pose, one two-handed sword attack pose and one javelin throwing pose.

For printing I think the large version will be fine on FDM but like my previous model you'll want to be mindful of the spear. There are a few small surfaces and horns so it could be a bit of pain when removing supports on FDM but I believe in you! Just make sure to rotate the model so that supports aren't wrapped around the spear and you should be good. For resin I think auto-supports will work completely fine - just tilt it back 45 degrees.
