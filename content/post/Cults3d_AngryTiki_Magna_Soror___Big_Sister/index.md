When the battle sisters need extra firepower, they call in the "Big Sister."  This is my custom knight for the sisterhood.

It's a mix of AI, kit bash, and a lot of work to make it posable.  All main parts are posable above the waist: torso, head, arms, weapons.  Comes with and without the skirt (separate object that is cut to fit under the crotch armor).  With or without the iron halo.
