15mm scale Havoc Brother Seargent of the Plague Brother detachment.

This is a leader model and is 10% larger than the standard Plague Bros. He has an assault rifle and plague scythe as his armament.

The scythe is a few mm off the build plate and oriented horizontally for maximum strength. I suggest CA superglue to attach it to the model.

I left these models as overlapping geometry instead of combining them with booleans so that others can more easily remix this work into other poses or new models entirely.
