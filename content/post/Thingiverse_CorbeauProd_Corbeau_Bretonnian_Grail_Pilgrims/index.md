Description :

~~~
To escort the Grail Reliquary, we needed some fanatic pilgrims. I wanted them to look like religious cultists, with some serious gesture and some stuff for in-battle devotions. I had in mind the picture of fat friars and fighter-monks in dresses and I wanted to be able to customize each minis but with a unique body basis.

The body I did has a very neutral standing which allows many custom stuff like a staff, a standard, a scroll, a sword, a club and of course, a shield. You can use any heads of my bretonnian set on it (the archers' hooded one, the errants' hairless one or even any of you own). For the picture, I did many pilgrims using legit heads, printed heads and even a greenstuff made head). The stuff used to custom is mainly handmade with toothpicks, greenstuff or comes from legit kits.

A body takes 1h15 to be printed.

You'll find the reliquary there:
https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4520462
~~~
