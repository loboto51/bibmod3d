Small kitbash of the nyx parts with a new torso to make some prototype proof of concept pilots for the hecate and thanatos. Only monopose because of this

The 2 seated models will need to be digitally kitbashed into the mech torsos of your choice, included at both 32mm and 28mm scale. The mechs will need to be scaled to 115% for the 32mm scale pilots but is the recommended scale to do them at

2 on foot pilots

5 helmets compatible with the nyx helmets as well

Getting to the point of making custom pilots with mechs was one of my main objectives when I started 3d modelling nearly 2 years ago now, so just thought I'd share these now I've finally met that goal :)
