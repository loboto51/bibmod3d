Dragon attack? Gnoll raiding party? A group of overpowered murderhobos causing a ruckus?
Whatever the reason, here are some fleeing peasants to add drama to your town scene! 
Available in coif+hat and bowl cut, and in both regular and burninated!

This mini is scaled to 28mm, floor to eyeline! If you want it bigger or smaller, feel free to scale it as you please! No base is included, but it is sized to fit on a 1 inch / 25.4mm diameter base. If you would like a 32mm version, scale up to 114.1%.
