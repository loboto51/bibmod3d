Big props to the originators of the files i remixed from!

These are conversion parts for turning regular Intercessors into Suppressors. 

I like the idea of what Suppressors can do but not how the official models look, super unwieldy guns, grav/jump packs not looking jump-packy enough, odd moon boots. 
(and those models are only available in a boxed set that also containing lots of stuff im not interested in) .

After looking at some conversions and kitbashes i decided to try doing a remix that would be more to my taste, this is what i came up with.

To use this one needs to cut away the Bolt Rifle from the right hand and scoop material out of the left hand to fit the handle (or replace said hand with an empty bolter left from a previous marine kit).

Ive used Dark Imperium Intercessors for mine but these parts should be usable on pretty much any of the Intercessor models holding their bolter with both hands.

Suppressors go on 40mm bases, here is one: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3312581
~~~
