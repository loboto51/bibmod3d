Are you too tired of never taking a toxicrene because its got a 10 inch wide hugging pose that's impossible to move between terrain or transport to your local games?  Have I got good news for you then!  I used the original tentacle arms and reposed them into a few different options.  

Pose 2 was mostly a joke, stretching the tentacles out forward instead of to the sides, still makes the toxi a bit unwieldy, but now front to back rather than side to side.  This pose works better with the body propped up on the tactical rock but can be used for the downward pose if you rest the tentacles on the table.

Pose 3 is just a slight repose of the stock tentacle pose, but they're rotated slightly upwards so that you don't have to prop him up on the raised arm and tactical rock (i.e. dead terminator)

Pose 4 has the tentacles arched backwards like they're ready to fling forward like giant whips

Pose 5 was the most work and my favorite, taking inspiration from octopus tentacles, has all the tentacles curled around and coiled making the arms much more compact.  This option makes the toxi fit almost fully on the proper base size.

Because I like the downward pose better than the propped up one, I also did some minor reposing of the face tentacles so they don't hit the base when in the lower pose.  

Thanks to XenoBits for the original files I worked with, you should checkout his page and get the original files from him. 

Raw and supported files included.
~~~
