~~~
The Griffin is a fast, light attack vehicle developed by squats engineers to combine the extreme mobility of their motorcycles and trikes with the ability to traverse impossible terrain. Equipped with gravitic repulsors, the Griffin hovers several meters above the ground, allowing it to move with ease over rocks, ruins, swamps, and even water.

Although it normally hovers at low altitude, the vehicle can briefly rise to about ten meters to perform rapid "jumps," overcome obstacles, or execute controlled descents from great heights. This capability, combined with its extraordinary maneuverability, allows the Griffin to perform tight turns, lateral movements, and high-speed lightning attacks. On flat terrain, it can reach speeds close to 250 km/h, while its silent repulsors make it an ideal platform for reconnaissance missions, incursions behind enemy lines, and ambushes.

The standard armament consists of a heavy laser cannon mounted behind the pilot for engaging vehicles or armored targets, supplemented by hull-mounted repeating lasers for eliminating light infantry. The primary weapon can be replaced with other heavy options such as bolters, plasma cannons, multi-meltas, or missile launchers. Unlike squat trikes, the Griffin employs an advanced automatic target acquisition system, capable of selecting and locking onto targets faster than many automated turrets. The pilot can monitor the system through their helmet, change targets with simple neural impulses, or assume manual control if the situation demands it.

Although its larger size makes it more conspicuous than squat bikes, in the hands of a skilled pilot, the Griffin's speed and agility make it a relentless hunter on the battlefield.
