~~~

I have the old Planetary Empires set for using in 40K campaigns, however, the set only contains random landscapes and Imperial ruins. For our dynamic campaigns, I am making simple faction specific tiles for some of the major armies.

Here is the second expansion set for the terrain tiles. Included are tiles that represent, but are not limited to, the Foothills, Alien Creep, Plains, and Mountainous terrain. Imagination and paint choices can have you making these appear as numerous different biomes and landscapes! If you require peg holes for flags and upgrades in any of the tiles missing them, just drill a 7/64" hole through the tile in the location you feel is best. As always, enjoy!
~~~
