Hi, made this a year ago but forgot to upload.
Have not cleaned them but they are support free and most have integrated bed supports.
I made the bottom 1mm of mine to have no top or bottom layers and made it a grid pattern on the infills so that it looks like a mesh.
I have not rotated them into the right orientation, you will have to do something yourself when it is FREE! ;)

They are prepared for Ø3x2mm magnets så that they snapp great together.
I used one orientation of them on the left side and the opposite on the right side of all connection surfaces.

They are optimized for storage so that you can stack them ontop of each other.

The grid pattern on the floors are almost 1" (24,5mm)

Do not mind getting a tip for 100h+ of work ;)

Have fun and enjoy!
