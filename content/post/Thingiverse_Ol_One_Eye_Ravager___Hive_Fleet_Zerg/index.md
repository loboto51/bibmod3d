~~~
Ravager from StarCraft 2.

These are my favorite models I have done, and the idea of painting these was the whole reason I started this project. These models were sculpted in Zbrush and poses were made with Blender. 

These models can fit onto a 60 mm base with a little bit of work. You will need to add some bits or small terrain pieces to allow the claws to stand on. These models are scaled to 1:64th. The Ravager can act as a proxy for either the Biovore or Pyrovore. 

I got great results using my Elegoo Mars 3 with the a mix of 30% Siraya Tech Tenacious and 70% Siraya Tech Fast. I used Siraya Tech's recommended setting for the Fast resin. You might want to increase the amount of Siraya Tech Tenacious to 30% - 35% to improve the flexibility and shock resistance of the print.
~~~
