Provides a version of Woddish's excellent Tiny Tank Classic MBT and alman69uk's additional weapons that have been sliced into major parts that are enabled to be quickly swapped and edited for customization by changing visibility of components between different options for turrets and weapons to make Predator based vehicles (Destructor, Annihilator, Baal, Infernus, and plasma deimos pattern tanks).
