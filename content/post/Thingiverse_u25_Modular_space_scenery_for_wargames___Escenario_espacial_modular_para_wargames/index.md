Tiles for modular spaceship/base/whatever scene for wargames (Warhammer 40k scale).
You will find 2 different wall designs with assymetric sides in order to get more variety. Each design have:
1 wall 1x1
4 walls 2x1
3 walls 3x1
Corners:
4 types of "L" corner
3 types of "T" corner
1 type of "X" corner
5 types of floors. Each type have:
2x1 tile
2x2 tile
2x3 tile
3x1 tile
3x3 tile
4 types of 1x2 doors and a 1x2 tile floor for them.
2 types of 1x1 doors and a 1x1 tile floor for them 
The doors are split for better print and needs to be glued.
 All parts have been designed to require no support, but some wall with pipes needs minor supports.
