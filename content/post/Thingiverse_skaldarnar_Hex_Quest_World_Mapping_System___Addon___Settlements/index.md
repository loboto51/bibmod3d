# Hex Quest Addon - Settlements

These are additional tiles for the [Hex Quest World Mapping System](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3728353) by [ClayRade](https://www.thingiverse.com/ClayRade/about).

I've printed the tiles at 0.08mm-0.1mm layer heigth with a print speed of 40-50mm/s (using a 0.4mm nozzle) on an Anycubic i3 Mega-S.

## Content

- **`settlements-ford-town.stl`** - a _town_ located in the elbow of a river, with a ford connecting the town to a road on the other side of the river
- **`settlements-mountain-tower.stl`** - a _watchtower_ in the mountains, reachable via a bridge over a river elbow
- **`settlements-plains-camp.stl`** - a _camp_ (tents in the style of centaurs/Dothraki) on a plain
- **`settlements-river-city.stl`** - a _city_ located at a straight river
