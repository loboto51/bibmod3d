~~~
Originally obtained from DimensionV on cults3d

https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/game/katarachnophron-kataphron-with-legs
https://cults3d.com/en/users/DimensionV/3d-models
https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4314709/files

As with the original model, parts are included from others, including
- Sand walker (28mm) by SebTheis on thingiverse
  https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:2882646
- Adeptus Mechanicus Token by MonkeyBob
  https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3042891



Like DimensionV i felt that our space technicians needed more legs. 
I how ever felt that only having four on such a small unit didnt feel 
quite right, and so i used 3d builder to modify the model to make it
have a feeling a little closer to that of some of the magos art ive
seen out there. 

I have included two versions of the completed frame, one to fit on a 60mm
base and the other to fit a 65mm or larger base. the reason for this 
is the way i built it, it doesnt quite fit a regular 60mm base. I have also
included the more general sections, such as the abdomen, core, core with legs, 
and the leg unit, but im not sure how easily the legs will fit during 
manual assembly.
~~~
