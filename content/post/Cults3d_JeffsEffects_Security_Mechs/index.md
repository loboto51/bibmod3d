We are passionate about creating cutting-edge solutions that enhance safety and security. Our autonomous security robots are designed to safeguard communities, businesses, and public spaces, combining advanced technology with real-world effectiveness. These tireless sentinels patrol areas, detect anomalies, and provide valuable data to security teams, while also providing cheap, effective security and guard duty when human resources are in short supply. Don't leave quick security decisions to biased and impressionable guards. Our security bots will follow your programming unquestionably and will make the right call every time. 

Security Mechs
Miniatures for Grimdark Future and other tabletop war games. All models are scaled to 32mm to be compatible with SW Legion line of miniatures.

Models included:
Loki Mech
Fenris Mech
Ymir Mech

Pre-supported versions of the models are also provided. However, I'm not the best at creating supports. They work, and all of the models shown were printed with the supported version, but they could use some improvement. Be very carful removing supports as there are lots of very delicate pieces. The Loki Mechs especially have some very thin parts. I highly suggest cutting away these areas then trimming flush.

Decals:
Also included is a copy of the decal graphics I created for the miniatures. The images were created by me based on the original textures. I printed the decals on an Alps MD-1300 printer using spot colors. Below is a list of the colors used and the process.
Printed using Elephant's Rocket Printer Utility at 1200 x 600 dpi spot colors
Not White -> Special White
Black -> Black
R:150	G:152	B:154 -> Black (Elephant's Rocket Mid Gray)
R:238	G:61	B:105 -> Black (Elephant's Rocket Red)
R:238	G:61	B:105 -> Black (Elephant's Rocket Orange)
(NOTE: I use the TIF format for printing, but had to convert it to PNG to upload. So be aware that the color values may have changed a little in the conversion.)

Credit:
