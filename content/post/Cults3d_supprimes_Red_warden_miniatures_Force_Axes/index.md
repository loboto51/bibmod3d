Description :

~~~
I wanted to try some more complicated boole operations and the squiggly lines and curved edges of force weapons seemed like they could be done like that. 

It's just the great force axe from the BA terminator librarian but with a generic librarius emblem instead of a drop of blood. The smaller axe is just a quick remix of the other one.

I'm not sure how feasible it is to print cables and bend them slightly afterwards so I've made a version with and without. The cable should be long enough to reach the back of a marine with the arm extended at least. 

Edit: Now with proper hands and 5 times lighter
~~~
