Description :

~~~
I imagine these guys as the juveniles of my previous fungal aliens, not quite fully grown yet. But what they lack in size and experience, they make up for in attitude.

Contains 6 goofy little aliens with a variety of melee weapons and guns. At the default size (meant for 15-18mm scale) they are about 11-13mm tall. The weapons and pouches are not merged into the hands/bodies, so you can easily separate them for easy digital kitbashing!

I have test printed them, and pre-supported files are included!
