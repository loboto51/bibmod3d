I made a very basic base for this guy so that it can be used at any scale. I also made a terrain piece to match the base the 'Toxic Pond'!

The mech comes with 10 different slot-on, interchangeable arm weapon options, 5 different slot-on, interchangeable chest weapons. Multiple leg poses, ect
