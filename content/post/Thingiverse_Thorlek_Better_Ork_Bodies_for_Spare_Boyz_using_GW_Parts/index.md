~~~
This is a remix/tweak of my previous remix including parts from Graygawr to make the Vidovicarts Custom Ork Bodies fit GW Ork Arms/Heads/Accessories.

I fixed the shoes so they have a sole, i lowered the shoulders and lifted the necks so the head position was better, and then i messed around for ages getting the armour from GrayGawr to fit nicely on the bodies.


In the end, I printed them at 0.12mm Layer Hight on my Ender 3 and they work really well with GW Ork Parts.


To use them with Nobz Arms/Heads they just need to be another 15% or so bigger


The original bodies are not in thingiverse, buy you can find them here and support to original creator:
https://www.myminifactory.com/object/3d-print-custom-orks-2020-pack-1-138838
~~~
