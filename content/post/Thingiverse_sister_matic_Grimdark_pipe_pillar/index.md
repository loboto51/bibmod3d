Description :

~~~
So you have some Thermic Plasma Conduits™ (or locally sourced alternative with the same dimensions), but rather than having them pretty much on the ground, you want them a bit higher to make things interesting. Say, with a centre-line height of 61mm off the table, which is exactly the right height to match up with the mid-line of several Sector Mechanicus™ components, how about that?

Well, have I got good news for you: I had this exact requirement and made myself a pillar type thingy that has a good deal of visual congruity with Sector Mechanicus™ pillars. 

Honestly, there's not much more to say. The pipe foot bit itself is very simple and is not imitating the originals, but it does the same job and has very similar tolerances - it's just snug enough that I can pick up the pipe and the pillars come with, but aren't jammed in.

I have also included a basic pipe foot without the pillar, in case you have other stuff on the ground and want it to match. Lots of people have made pipe feet that do this job though, so take it or leave it!

The supported files (ending in "_sup") are what I printed with and was happy with. YMMV. I am definitely not an expert.
~~~
