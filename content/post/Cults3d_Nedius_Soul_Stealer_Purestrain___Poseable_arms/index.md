Haunting the depths of desolate hulks drifting through space, Soul Stealers are a vile breed of alien monstrosity feared for their ability to psychically control their victims, leaving them paralyzed and unable to defend themselves from the Soul Stealers razor-sharp claws, or leaving them vulnerable to becoming a slave-host for the Soul Stealers genetic material.

This model is inspired by a classic grim-dark xenos breed from some of my favourite board games growing up.
