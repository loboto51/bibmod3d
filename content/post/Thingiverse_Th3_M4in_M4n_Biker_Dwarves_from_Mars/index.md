With Toftylicious´s consent, Im uploading my custom models of his motorcycle, modified to fit MrOrangeJumper´s riders as much as i can make possible with 3D Builder.

I have added my personal touch in the form of a small luggage rack on the rear wheel and  footrests, so that the rider does not look weird with his feet on air.

I offer the model in its two variants, biker and chopper (maybe I will add a trike in the future), but also in three versions each: 
1) Just as I personalized and printed them, with details and accessories 
2) Without accessories on the motorcycle and the rider, but with my additions. 
3) Same as before, but the rider without head, so that you can use the heads that come in the Darguth´s Space Dwarf Berserkers - Builder Kit.

Maybe if I have a little more time I'll make some rider heads.

And I have added a 28mm base for the bikes. It might be a little small for the chopper, but overall it looks good.
~~~
