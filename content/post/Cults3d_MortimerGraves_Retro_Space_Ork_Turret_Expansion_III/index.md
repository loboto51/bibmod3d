Description :

~~~
A small addition to my wife's retro Rogue Trader and Second Edition Space Ork army, this is a 28mm compatible interpretation of the old Epic Ork Custom Battlewagon Turret #2 built from scratch in Tinkercad. This variant seems to have been a bit rare and I could only find a few different images of the Epic original.

The mortar is truly ridiculous! I scaled it down very slightly so that in-scale it would *only* be an 800mm gun.

It is designed to simply drop into the troop compartment of my [Retro Space Ork Battlewagon (Redux)](https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/game/retro-space-ork-battlewagon-redux-mortimergraves) or  original [Retro Space Ork Battlewagon](https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/game/retro-space-ork-battlewagon), turning it into a Gunwagon, and can be removed when you want to use the vehicle as a transport.

It may need a bit of jiggling to get past the "tubes" to the rear of the troop compartment but otherwise goes in and out easily.
~~~
