An ogryn with a shock baton and combat shield for Warhammer 40k or OPR.

I started wargaming with 40k. Still have my cadian army and dust it off once or twice a year, but rarely play. I discovered OPR a couple of weeks ago, and I like how quick it is to play a Firefight match. I could even print a small band of the faction I always wanted, since I have seen a trooper in a catalog: Praetoria. After playing some games against myself, I decided I need an ogryn. So I made one. Not printed yet, but I am eager to get him on the battlefield already.

Though I liked the 4th edition design as well, I think nothing beats the rogue trader ogryns.

Base from https://www.printables.com/model/1443237-complete-40k-base-pack
