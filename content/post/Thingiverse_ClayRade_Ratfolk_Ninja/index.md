It is modeled toward the more "heroic" side as far as scale goes, so if it pleases you, feel free to scale it down. It will fit on a 25.4mm / 1 inch diameter round base (not included). Three different weapon loadouts are included; one with a Tanto and Wakizashi (swords), one with Katars (punching daggers), and one with Kamas (hand sickles).
