I have been releasing some free models to help support the community during this weird time we live in, with uncertain economic outlooks and lots of free time. I have been releasing some free scifi stuff from my current KS (banner link above) but wanted to include some stuff for all the fantasy lovers.

[Full fantasy freebies collection here](https://www.thingiverse.com/ecaroth/collections/ec3d-fantasy-freebies)

This release is a Rock Golem from the deep places of the world - magically brought to life, covered in carved runic lines and crystalline formations. Should print well on FDM or resin, but please note that this guy *does* require some supports (under right fist, chin, and possibly crotch).

This model is from one of my previous Kickstarters, The Skyless Realms, full of all manner of races and beasties from the below - if you want to check out more like this the Kickstarter is available for late pledge (link below). Keep an eye out for more free stuff from me shortly!
