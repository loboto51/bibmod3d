Description :

~~~
This is a remix of Tilinger's amazing skeletons, so credit goes to him.  I just customized the "strips".  I never liked how certain armies are modelled in perfect file rows.  This is my attempt at more of an undead horde with no real organization, just a mass of animated corpses.  Changes I made to the originals:
-slight scale down, think maybe 5% to bring it more in line with my other miniatures.
-remixed another 20+ individual skeletons with some new weapons for even more variety.
-put slight risers under the feet as the originals tended to get lost under any base texturing.  Now when you texture paint the base, if you're careful, the feet should appear on top of the ground instead of in it.

HIGHLY recommend a stronger resin for these (I used ABS-like) as those are some tiny bones.  Supports can be tricky, so I'm not even going to attempt to upload pre-supported as all machines are different and you'll have to probably deal with some failures before they come out perfect.  I found a slight angle on 2 axes worked best for me.

This is a work in progress and I intend to make 2-3 more full units worth before I'm finished.

**11/24 update:  
-The risers under the feet caused small gaps in between some of the weapon shafts that used to rest on the ground.  I attempted to fill these gaps so all files are updated.  
-Added 3rd unit
~~~
