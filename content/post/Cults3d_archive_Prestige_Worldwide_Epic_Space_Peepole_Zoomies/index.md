~~~
All credit for the creation of and permission to distribute these models goes to sentinel3 https://cults3d.com/en/users/sentinel3/3d-models. He not only allowed me to heavily modify and provide them to you for free, but allowed the medium bird to be provided which is not publicly available otherwise. 

I have taken the original designs and spent dozens of hours assembling, deleting, filling, holing, and preparing these designs for 1 piece printing at a tiny scale. Some things like the big guns and fins were thickened but the front end of the big bird is significantly different than the original. The angle has been lowered and the entirety lengthened. If you are unhappy with that change you are welcome to download the original from sentinel and modify it yourself.

Inside you find both hunter and raider variant of the big bird and normal, ace, and no gun variants of the medium bird. They are all provided in 8mm scale and 6mm scale versions. They are provided without base holes and with hawk widget (standard 3mm) holes. Additionally the 8mm versions also have additional models with holes cut for notaeronautica ball joint. The medium bird is also provided with the ventral cannon separately should you wish to kitbash in your own belly gun.

Hope to see some people paint these up for their collections. Enjoy!
~~~
