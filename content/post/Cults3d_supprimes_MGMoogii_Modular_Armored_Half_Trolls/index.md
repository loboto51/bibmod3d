Armored Half Trolls

The Lore: They are unnatural, artificial creatures that was created by combining orcs and trolls. Since the Great War that brought devastating loss on the greenskin kind and with the death of magic and sorcery that caused blight to greenskin reproduction effectiveness, weakening the spores strength. Orcs no longer can rely on their ignorance and numbers anymore. They needed to adapt to the new world and its systems if they wanted to survive. They are no longer numerous, but among all green skinned creatures, the troll numbers have suffered the most, due to their physical size and unique physiology they just could not reproduce as fast as their other kin and became rare sight to see. To compensate for the loss the orc army started experimenting different ways to increase their numbers.

After many failed attempts or accidents, they have found their solution. Dead troll carcass last much longer than many others, but it releases very toxic and flammable acid and gas that is deadly even for orcs, some of them can blow up if a fire were to be intreduced pretty early stage of decompostion, but after a year or so, if the carcass is not cleaned or burned, it turns that spot of land into an small pond of muddy swamp with all the smell and bugs included. In time the orcs found out that this small little pond can be very fertile soil for orc spores. In here spores managed to reach maturity much faster and much reliable compare to everywhere else, just like the old days, but sometimes in very rare occasions among the newborn orcs something different and larger came out of the mud and these new creatures were later called half trolls, due to their size. 

Half troll are lot smaller in size compared to the original Trolls but still large and strong enough to pick up an orc and throw him over a cliff. But unlike the trolls, they still have some intact orcish intellect left in them. They have verbal, linguistic skill and capability to understand basic military tactic like defending and charging, but their violant and aggressive nature makes them difficult to control. 

In this pack you can create 3 types of units. Rock thrower for range, Weapon wielder for defense and Armored brawler for charge.
