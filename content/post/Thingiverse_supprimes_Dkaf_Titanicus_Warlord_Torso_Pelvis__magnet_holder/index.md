~~~
Just a small holder to easily magnetize the AT Warlord Torso to its Pelvis. Not mandatory, but this part always felt wobbly to me when handling the mini.

This needs to be mounted before gluing the hip platform to the torso. On the pict I printed a halfpart to test the depth and see the clearance for the magnets.

Designed to hold two 5mm diameter, 1 mm thick round magnets.
~~~
