(These parts and all my previous listings of Armoured Space Dwarves have been reworked and updated from what they originally were, with the whole model being scaled up by 1.1x, the hands and heads enlarged and the feet reduced in height slightly. The belt buckle detail has also changed; with the rune on the originals changed for a five sided twist release devise. This is the easiest way to check from which version any previously downloaded models are from and if they need to be replaced)

I present my fifth set of armoured space dwarf helmets. These are a few new designs that I felt were worth including.
First is an Astronaut style helmet; for sort of an old Squat and Votann middle-ground, inspired by this image: www.thecommguild.com/gallery/767558-Squats%20and%20Jokaero.html
Second is the Combat helmet, which is meant to look a little closer to a space marine helmet, at least the top half.
Third are a couple of helmets for some upcoming musician models, which will be this army's version of a Chaplain. I think their influence is fairly clear.
Last are the Fighter helmets, which initially were meant to be an expansion of the musician thing, but these should work with standard units.
There are 27 new helmets included here.
These are for use on my armoured space dwarves army, which (apart from the helmets) will look like very short space marines.

These helmets have a different body interface than my earlier versions and to fit them into my Armoured Space Dwarf models will require the V2 Helmet Neck Spacer part. The old design didn't allow the heads to look to the side properly, so this change corrects that.

These models are scaled to match in with the original metal Squat models. To match the scale of other currently available Space Dwarf STL files; these models will need to be scaled up by 1.1x.

Heads still to be made are any special character helmets, like a Medic, Living Ancestor or Guild Master.
~~~
