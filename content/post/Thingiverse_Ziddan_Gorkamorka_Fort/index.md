~~~
This is a 3d printable recration of the cardboard fort that came with the 1997 release of Gorkamorka.

Prints with quite minimal support requirements, except for the gate arch.

The printed version shown is at half scale, printed on a Mars 2 for my half scale Gorkamorka gang.

Gallery of the fort from different directions > http://imgur.com/a/RPKxdof

Ive got other Gorkamorka recreations, the Turny Gubbin, Rebel Grot Big Lugga, Rebel Grot Cutta as well as the tower that came in the boxed set.

I plan to remix a stand in for mine entrance in the future.

The remix linking feature is dead atm but it is using parts from:
https://www.thingiverse.com/sirbats/designs
https://www.thingiverse.com/terrain4print/designs
https://www.thingiverse.com/wastelandscenics/designs

Huge shout out to those creators!
