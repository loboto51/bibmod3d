Description :

~~~
28mm scale model for war gaming. (1/56 scale, heroic scale)

Reverse engineered from alien technology, the Mercenary Guass Rifle features a combined battery and ammunition pack, a commercial off the shelf scope, and a grip and stock taken from the popular export pulse carbines. Communications equipment was removed from this model, making it lighter, and more ideal for those acting alone.

For added stability, it is recommended to use a loop sling, or power armor. Bi-pods are too fragile.

Extended magazines and backpack generators are to be provided in the future.
~~~
