Note: This is fan-art, completely built from scratch by myself, polygon by polygon.

EDIT (28th Jan 2026): I have now uploaded new updated xenos (type1 and type2 variants). They've gone through a minor facelift and some improvements here and there, like better tail deformations etc.

This set contains 22 different alien creature poses with two head variants, made for miniature gaming purposes. About 32mm scale by default. Easily printed in smaller scales as well. Also works as decorations. Purely fan-made material. Everything is 100% my own creation.

This is my take on the classic acid blooded monsters with banana shaped heads. I have sculpted, rigged and posed them myself. I just wanted to have an alternate set to play my Space Hulk and didn't find any printable minis that would have suited my needs, so I made my own. Hope you like em. :D The human minis in pictures are from Papsikels patreon, which I'm also a huge fan of.

I've printed mine with an Prusa Mini+ FDM printer, in reduced 70% scale (to fit 20mm grid). They are designed to be beefy enough without too fiddly parts, so they should be pretty easy to print, especially in standard sizes. A friend also printed a couple with his resin printer - turned out great! (Pictures with the red background.)

If you print them in 80% scale they should work pretty great with GF9's Aliens minis.
