Description :

~~~
So sexy, So sleek, So very deadly. This here is my remix of BloodyLeechs' Grimdark Chrono Trigger Bits. After I had printed it and assembled it, I just, really didnt like it. It just didnt look right. So I gave it a little attention. I removed the tank on the back and in its place put a Reactor with logo. I got the reactor part from AntomanElven and his Back reactor extension bit. I then put a port hole for the new shoulder stubber and mount, which I aquired from https://www.patreon.com/themakerscult. Then I added a logo to the skirt plate which I also got from AntomanElven. I then replaced the head with one from SebTheis' Dominion Crusader head pack. I included 2 head poses. After that I replaced the arms with well, cooler arms which I got from https://www.patreon.com/ghamak. Lastly I replaced the legs. Oh God I hated those legs. So I took the legs from one of my favorite robots of all time, ED209 and welded them on. Now, she's a thing of beauty.

As soon as I have some more time I am going to work on a fist variant. In the mean time I hope you enjoy.
~~~
