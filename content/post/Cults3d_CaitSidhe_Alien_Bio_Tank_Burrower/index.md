An old school alien bio tank for sci fi games in 28mm scale. This large snakey variant is intended to burrow under enemies defences and create opening behind enemy lines and massacre enemy troops with it's huge bladed arms and burrowing jaws. 

A scaled down full version is included with the larger scale split model though obviously size it to your own uses.

On the large version the arms are separate, one of each arm is provided and can be mirrored in your slicer software to get the opposite number. Supports will be required and likely requires a 200mm print bed to print the main body.
