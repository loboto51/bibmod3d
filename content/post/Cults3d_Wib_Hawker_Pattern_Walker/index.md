*"The Hawker Pattern Walker is a slightly larger variant of an existing chassis used by many Imperial commanders. Pilots have stated on record that they enjoy its greater manoeuvrability, easier weapon swaps, and increased leg room. Although they are less enthused by its propensity to explode, something we hope to rectify in the next revision."*

The Hawker comes with an array of different weapons for both the wings and belly, 3 different leg poses, and 3 different foot poses. Alternatively I have included a leg with foot included if you don’t want to faff around posing it.

If you would like to put this on a base it will need an 80mm one if printed at the default size.

The legs are all the same side so remember to mirror one so you have a matching pair. You will also need to mirror the missile launcher if you want to take 2 of them.
