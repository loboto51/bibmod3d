Description :

~~~
Hey folks! Just 3 parts for you to place in your miniatures to customize your characters. Extra thick to increase compatibility. Heavily based in historic milanese armor (high waist large shoulder piece, few folds)
My process to adapt them (blender 2.8+):
1- Load your miniature and the armor parts
2- Place them more or less in position
3- Get the best fit you can simply scaling it (if some parts of the mini stick out, don't worry, get the overall placement)
4 - Try sculpting down (elastic deform brush in blender works wonders!) the parts sticking out in the original mini.
5- Use the sculpting elastic deform to give the final touches. 

It normally takes less than 5 min for even somewhat complex models!

Hope you enjoy it!
~~~
