I thought the time has come to fill a missing link within the glorious warlord designs of the Adeptus Titanicus.
The MK2 pattern was an intermediate model between the 1988 AT Beetleback Warlord and the Lucius pattern Epic Armageddon model.
(https://wh40k-de.lexicanum.com/wiki/Warlord_Kampftitan see german lexicanum article)

The model should be printed at 75% for Adeptus Titanicus scale.

This is my very first model with a 3d model programm.
This war made from scratch by looking at the picture. :)
Please be merciful with your critique. ;)

I added a "solid" STL due to manifould issues. (Edit: The solid file gets lost in thingiverse atm, sorry for that)
The untreated STL file also includes a highly WIP exhaust and the bare Shoulder/Carapace bit.
A prototype testprint is on my printerbed. I will add the photos soon.

Please feel free to modify, remix and correct the model as you wish!
~~~
