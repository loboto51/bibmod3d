Chaos Cultist #11 Heavy Specialist with BIG Stubber

Recently I released "Chaos Cultist #1" and now I'm releasing this big burly brute with a big gun. And you ask, "What happened to Chaos Cultists #2 through #10?" Well, I skipped them for now. I wanted to make a standout cultist that had a bit more gravitas then just a bunch more basic cultists. I do plan to release Chaos Cultists #2 through #10 and more, just no promises on what order they arrive in.

[UPDATE 2023-06-19] I wanted to fine tune a few things on this miniature. I increased the size of the Big Stubber's barrel because it looked to thin and wimpy on the tabletop to me. I raised the height of the two wrenches on the miniature's backpack so that they have more support and should be more sturdy. I've printed them fine before, but if I'm in a hurry when removing the supports I have also torn off the ends. I also added a bit of thickness to the ammo belt that hangs from the Big Stubber (it's on the backside and not noticeable, paint it like the links in the ammo belt). I've printed models with much thinner parts including ammo belts, but I would like to make my designs as robust and hassle free as possible. Enjoy.
