In the modern days, the mighty magicians of the Magnusons have to control their once glorious elite warriors. With the pass of centuries they've turned into mad-boys obsessed in scarabs, well they also lost their bodies but that's not important... They have to be controlled to stay focused on the battlefield and fight with their brothers until all the dust has gone.

This kit includes several pieces to convert your modern dusty scarab armored warriors and give them their glorious old days flavour.

Parts included (everything tartarus compatible):

- 5 torsos with heads
- 3 tabards from different cabals
- kopeshi combat weapons: axe and stave for band leader
- infernal shooting combi-weapon
- decorating bits for band leader: cape, stoles, spell book and enchantment scrolls
- shoulders in 2 designs and with 3 cabal symbols
- special shoulder for the band leader with 3 cabal symbols
- infernal missile rack adapter
- rotatory barrel to convert the warping flamer into a reaping cannon

As all my files, all the parts are presupported.
