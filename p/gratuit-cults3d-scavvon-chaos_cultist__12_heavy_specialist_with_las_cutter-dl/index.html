I'm making this series of miniatures to be Chaos Culitsts, however, without too much trouble you could use them to depict Sneaky Infiltrating Xeno-morphs. In fact, I've been playing the new Murder Squad a bit lately and while I like the look and the rules for the Bloody Treasonous Soldiers, they don't feel "chaos cultist" enough for me. So I'm going to run a Murder Squad of Sneaky Infiltrating Xeno-morphs which will 'count-as' Chaos Cultists. Hence, the reason why this fellow has a Las-cutter. Hey, Chaos Cultists come from all social strata of Imperial citizens too!

[UPDATE 2023-06-21]: Just like on Chaos Cultist #11 which I updated recently, I have moved the two wrenches on his backpack upwards so they are better supported and sturdier.  I also made the battery cables on the las-cutter thicker. I think they look better and should be more sturdy.
