Veteran Sergeant of the XVI Legion in Mark 3 Armour equipped with a Powerfist and a Umbra pattern Combi-plasma.
If you need more Combi-plasma's like this one find them here; 
https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/game/combi-long-choom-umbra

For the most part I used Helicopterhelicopters MKIII kits as a bases and got his permission to share these!
Find his  awesome kits here; https://cults3d.com/en/users/HelicopterHelicopter/3d-models

I used parts from Red Warden Miniatures and sculpted the rest myself.

All the parts are PRESUPPORTED and test printed! The body, arms, head and other parts are separated for ease of printing.

Edit 1: Added the unsupported version of the original and a variation with a Plasma pistol.
