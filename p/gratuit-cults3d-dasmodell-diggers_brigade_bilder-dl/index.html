Greetings after many weeks! Finally, I am glad to present you the first wargame miniature based on the new setting. From this moment on, I step on thin ice, switching to artistic descriptions, being not a specialist in the mastery of words, and also not being an English-speaking person. I will try not to disappoint my subscribers and share the details of the setting as much as possible. I hope in the future I will be able to create an English-language resource where you can fully appreciate the idea, but for now keep a small piece that specifically affects these guys:

"The main source of raw materials for the alchemical industry of the Diamute people are ubiquitous deep layers of rock - necrolite formed by the remains of creatures of an ancient civilization.

The ancients were beings of a high order, capable of using magic, including flesh magic. With her help, the Haruspices, magicians of the flesh, built entire cities from the bodies and souls of specially grown donors (thus formed a significant part of the necrolite known today). Particularly guilty slaves and criminals also became donors of bodies for the research of Haruspices, which was a rather cruel and grotesque type of execution. Now the remnants of this civilization, known to humans as demons, inhabit the Bowels - an unlimited labyrinth of underground structures built by the Ancients.

The incessant mining of necrolite deposits often leads to clashes between miners and demons. The model you see represents such a getter. His life support suit is designed for long, deep expeditions into the Bowels. The power circuits are saturated with alchemical solutions that endow miners with supernatural endurance, but adversely affect their minds and souls. Armor plates and a sophisticated frame allow miners to withstand heavy loads of heavy and dangerous work with necrolite, as well as resist groups of younger demons waiting for miners in the endless spaces of the Bowels."
