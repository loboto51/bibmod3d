This is a digital 3D printable model of a Mage. Or a king. It’s up to you. The heads, hands, chests, arms, and legs are completely modular and you can switch them as you progress in your character’s story and equip it with different weapons, spells, and accessories.

**The download contains:**
4x head
4x legs
1x base
3x chest
6x arms
Hands wielding different objects
Accessories such as a cloak and a backpack

All pieces of the model are ready to be 3D printed on an SLA printer. They are prepared for inserting cylindrical magnets.

**Magnets we use:**
Diameter 2 mm
Height 0.90 mm

**SIZE**
At 100% scale the mage’s dimensions are approximately:
Width: 35 mm
Height: 40 mm
Base diameter: 25mm
Neck diameter: 3.10 mm
Lower body diameter: 6.30 mm

You can scale the model as you wish.

**ASSEMBLY**
The holes in the models are prepared for inserting magnets (2mm diameter, 0.90 mm high)

This model is meant to be post-processed after printing. This means inserting magnets, and painting.
Printing settings will vary greatly depending on your specific printer.

Learn how to cut your own figures and prepare them for magnets in our Youtube tutorial:
https://youtu.be/A6QtOraIAfQ
