"The drenched wetlands of Cerabo IV poses many dangers, after a millenia of fighting, the once abundant flora of this world has turned into a toxic nightmare. 
Only with the right equipment do soldiers still march across the battlefield, to deliver justice to their enemies."


Updated 19-11-2020:
since so many people seem to like my remix, I've added a few more variants to the roster.
including a... WIP? female sergent, using the head of Toawi's Soldier Female: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4596304/files

the different body options has the heads facing FL(left), FR(right), FF(front).

A remix of LoggyK's torso and kasrkin helmet, combined into one, and also a standalone backpack of my own doing. 
I pumped up the armour pieces a bit, to make them stand out more, and added a skull by Der_Graue
https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:2396869

Included is also a radio version, which can fit a 0.5mm wire piece as an antennae. 

The skull on the front has a small slope to help with printing.
