Have you ever looked at the beloved Angry Spaceguard sets created by Ziocru, LoggyK, and others, and thought to yourself "Well, yes, these guys are ANGRY, but surely they could be equally angry and ALSO fancy?" 

Well worry no more! Now, thanks to myself, the aforementioned creators, and a borrowed horse from Andy Woodhead over on Sketchfab, your angry boys can be the fanciest (yet still angry) men in all of space! 

Or, well, EVENTUALLY they will be. Right now, I'm mostly uploading these because I have to put some files SOMEWHERE as backup while I try to get my computer fixed, so here's as good a spot as any. Just, you know, Caveat Emptor because this is still very much a Work-In-Progress set.


----

V 0.2 update: Added new lower bodies, adjusted torsos, put Aquilas on hats and buttons. Replaced the Andy Woodhead horse with one of the 28mm Horses At Ease by BREXIT. 

And many thanks to Ben Antilles for reminding me that I needed to take a second look at those legs. 

Note that the heads may be a bit too big right now, if they are, set them to size 0.75.
