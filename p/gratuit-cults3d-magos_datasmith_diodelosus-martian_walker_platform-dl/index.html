Fresh from Forge World Diodelosus, the Walker Platform unit enables heavy guns to be swiftly moved over tricky terrain while elevated. Sporting two heavy weapons and a pintle-mounted gun, this vehicle can really do some damage!

This variant comes with options for the Laser-Blade or Lance-Beam as the main weapons and a heavy duty double-barrelled macrowave fusillade for the top.

Furthermore, it also comes with 3 shoulderplate options and 2 leg plate options.
