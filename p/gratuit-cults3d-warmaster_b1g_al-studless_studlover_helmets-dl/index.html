First of all thanks to Fummelfinger for allowing me to alter his free designs & upload them! 

Today I'm presenting you my collection of studless studlover helmets! I always liked the apperance of the MK V helmets but felt they could look cooler without their iconic bonding studs. So I removed them. Only the altered helmets of mine are included in this pack, if you want the vanilla variants from Fummel you will need to check him out: 
https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/game/studlover-helmet-variants

The only exceptions are variants 5 & 6 since I kitbashed them to have a bit more variety. I hope you enjoy! 

The files were checked with 3D Builder and should be fine. I would also like to note that the current version is NOT supported. They might be supported in future versions but no promises. Slightly angled with light/medium autosupports should do the trick anyway!
