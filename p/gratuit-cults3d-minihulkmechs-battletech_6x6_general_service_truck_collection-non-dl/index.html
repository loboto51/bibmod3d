My next contribution to  Wolk's big collab - United Mecha Creators Project. Here's my collection of 6x6 General Service Trucks for your BattleTech games. They can easily represent the base variant of the 10 Ton [Flatbed Truck](https://www.sarna.net/wiki/Flatbed_Truck). Great for convoy escort missions or just as scatter terrain! 

There is 6 different 6x6 trucks: 
- Cargo Truck with dropsides
- Cargo Truck with canopy
- Flatbed Cargo Truck
- Tanker Truck
- Container Truck
- Container Truck - unladen
Ther'es also a shipping container and 2 pallets of cargo for them to transport. 

These are scaled for BattleTech (approx 6mm), but they can easily be scaled up for other table top games, since they're not very Sci-Fi looking.
