This is the "toolkit" file for Sinners.
It's in *.3mf format to keep size low and is the most modular save-file I can offer you to play with. Create your own models from bits or modify my release in whatever way you want. Please copy&paste the credits I added here and in the archive's text file and if you want to support, feel free to direct your generousity to any of the source files' authors. If you create something from this, please credit the original authors and myself properly and
spread your work free of charge.
If you want the easy-to-print version that comes with presupported files as well, please check out: https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/game/space-nuns-sinners

Thank you very much, Oz.
