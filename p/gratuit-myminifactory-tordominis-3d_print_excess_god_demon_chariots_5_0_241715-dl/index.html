The Excess God: Demon Chariots 5.0 bundle delivers a brutal and decadent vision of war at 10mm scale, featuring three distinct demon chariot models forged to embody speed, excess, and merciless destruction on the battlefield. Each chariot is designed with aggressive silhouettes, dynamic motion, and exaggerated details that remain crisp and readable even at small scale, making them ideal centerpieces for dark fantasy or infernal-themed armies.

Every model in this bundle is carefully optimized for high-quality resin printing. All miniatures come manually pre-supported using Lychee Slicer, with supports placed by hand to ensure reliable prints, minimal cleanup, and maximum preservation of fine details. The geometry is engineered to reduce unnecessary islands while maintaining structural strength, allowing for efficient and consistent printing across multiple copies.
